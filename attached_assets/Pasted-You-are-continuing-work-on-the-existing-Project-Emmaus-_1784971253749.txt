=====================================================
SPRINT NAME

Member Home and Next-Step Engine

=====================================================
SPRINT GOAL

Create a calm, trustworthy member experience centred on “Walk with Jesus.”

The user should never need to wonder:

“What do I do next?”

Emmaus should remember where the user stopped, understand what is available today, and present the most appropriate next step clearly.

The experience should feel like Emmaus is always one step ahead, ready for the user’s next need, without becoming controlling, demanding or guilt-driven.

This sprint is for testing the member experience.

Do not begin the Rooms redesign after completing it.

=====================================================
PROJECT PURPOSE

Emmaus exists because we believe technology can serve a faithful purpose when it is designed and used wisely to help people walk with Jesus Christ.

Emmaus is not a general AI chatbot.

Its purpose is to help people:

- Know Jesus
- Read Scripture
- Pray
- Grow as disciples
- Participate in church
- Serve others

The order of priority is:

1. Jesus
2. Scripture
3. Discipleship
4. Church
5. Technology

Technology exists to serve discipleship, not the other way around.

=====================================================
NON-NEGOTIABLE MEMBER EXPERIENCE PRINCIPLES

1. The app should always make the next meaningful step clear.

2. The user should trust that Emmaus remembers where they stopped.

3. Progress should encourage, never shame.

4. No XP.

5. No levels.

6. No leaderboards.

7. No competition.

8. No spirituality score.

9. No guilt-based streak language.

10. No “overdue” discipleship language.

11. No unnecessary decisions before the user reaches today’s content.

12. Scripture must remain central.

13. Ask Emmaus remains available but does not replace real discipleship.

14. Daily content should feel steady, calm and trustworthy.

15. The interface should feel simple, warm, modern and uncluttered.

=====================================================
NEXT-STEP ENGINE

Implement a reliable next-step recommendation engine.

It must be deterministic and understandable.

Do not use random recommendations.

Do not require unrestricted AI decision-making for the primary recommendation.

Use stored user progress, date availability, Journey status and assignments.

Recommended priority order:

1. Today’s unfinished 15 Minutes with Jesus session, especially for new users.

2. Today’s unfinished devotional.

3. The next unfinished step in the active Journey the user most recently continued.

4. A newly assigned church or Room Journey.

5. This week’s sermon if not yet listened to or opened.

6. An incomplete prayer, journal reflection or action step from recent content.

7. Another active Journey that has not been continued recently.

8. A suitable recommendation to begin a new Journey only when the user has available active-Journey capacity.

If a higher-priority item is unavailable or already complete, proceed to the next valid option.

Only one item should occupy the main Ready for you card.

Other available content should remain accessible below it.

The engine must remember:

- Exact Journey
- Exact Step
- Exact block or position where practical
- Completion status
- Last opened date
- Last continued date
- Daily content availability
- Paused or active state
- Room or church assignment

When the user taps Continue, take them directly to the correct next step rather than the Journey beginning or a generic overview screen.

- Walk with Jesus
- 15 Minutes with Jesus
- Daily devotionals
- This week’s sermon

- Made Free
- Marriage
- Parenting
- Volunteer Training
- Membership
- Bible Studies
- Prayer Journeys
- Youth Journeys
- Leadership Journeys
- Other long-form discipleship Journeys

A user may still:

- Preview unlimited Journeys
- Save unlimited Journeys for later
- Revisit completed Journeys
- Revisit paused Journeys

A user may only have two non-exempt Journeys marked active.

=====================================================
STARTING A THIRD JOURNEY

When a user tries to begin a third active growth Journey, do not block them with a harsh error.

Buttons:

Review my Journeys

Not now

- See current progress

- Continue one

=====================================================
DAILY CONTENT RULES

15 Minutes with Jesus and daily devotionals must follow local-calendar-day rules.

Use the user’s timezone where available.

Do not use a strict rolling 24-hour timer.

A new daily item becomes available on the next local calendar day.

Example:

If the user completes Day 1 at 10:00 PM, Day 2 becomes available after midnight in their timezone.

=====================================================
15 MINUTES WITH JESUS RULES

A user may progress through only one new Day of 15 Minutes with Jesus per local calendar day.

They may:

- Reopen today’s completed Day
- Review any previous Day
- Reread Scripture
- Repeat the reflection
- Repeat the prayer
- Review their own responses
- Listen again where audio exists

They may not:

- Unlock the following new Day on the same date
- Bypass the daily limit by reopening or refreshing
- Bypass the limit on another device
- Bypass the limit by changing the client-side clock where server-side validation is available

The server should validate daily progression where possible.

When complete, show:

Today’s time with Jesus is complete.

Come back tomorrow for the next step.

Do not use a countdown timer.

Do not show urgency.

=====================================================
DEVOTIONAL RULES

Daily devotionals use the same one-new-entry-per-local-day principle.

Users may:

- Read today’s devotional
- Reopen today’s devotional
- Revisit any previously unlocked devotional

Users may not:

- Open tomorrow’s devotional early
- Unlock future devotionals by rereading old entries
- Unlock several new devotional days at once

If users miss several days, present the next intended devotional calmly.

Do not show:

- Missed days
- Broken streaks
- Catch-up pressure
- Overdue devotional counts

=====================================================
OTHER JOURNEY PACING

Other Journeys are self-paced.

Users may:

- Complete several Steps in one day
- Complete one Step per day
- Pause for several days
- Revisit previous Steps
- Continue at their own pace

Do not impose daily locks on ordinary Journeys.

Emmaus may gently recommend that the user continue each day.

Examples:

Your next step in Made Free is ready.

Continue your Marriage Journey when you’re ready.

You can pick up where you stopped.

Never say:

You are behind.

You missed four days.

You have overdue Steps.

Your progress is too slow.
