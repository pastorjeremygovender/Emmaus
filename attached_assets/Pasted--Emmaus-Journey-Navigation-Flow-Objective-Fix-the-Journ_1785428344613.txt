# Emmaus - Journey Navigation Flow

## Objective

Fix the Journey navigation so it reflects the Emmaus hierarchy.

Current hierarchy:

Journey
↓
Walk
↓
Steps

The current implementation skips the Walk level.

---

## Current Behaviour

Next Steps

↓

Journeys

↓

Coming to Jesus

↓

Nothing happens (or navigates incorrectly).

This is incorrect.

---

## Required Behaviour

When the user taps a Journey card (for example, Coming to Jesus), open a Journey Details page.

Do not start a Walk.

Do not open Previous Days / Walk Contents.

---

## Journey Details Page

Display:

Journey Title

Example:

Coming to Jesus

Journey Description

Begin your walk with Jesus by discovering who He is, why He came and what it means to follow Him.

---

## Walks

Below the description, display every Walk that belongs to this Journey.

Example:

WHO IS GOD?

Discover who God is and why knowing Him changes everything.

Continue →

--------------------------------

CREATED IN GOD'S IMAGE

Discover your value and purpose.

Continue →

--------------------------------

WHAT WENT WRONG?

Understand how sin entered the world and why we all need a Saviour.

Continue →

--------------------------------

JESUS SAVES

Discover God's answer to our greatest need.

Continue →

Continue displaying every Walk assigned to that Journey.

---

## Selecting a Walk

Only when the user taps a Walk should Emmaus open that Walk.

The flow becomes:

Journey

↓

Walk

↓

Walk Introduction

↓

Steps

↓

Walk Complete

---

## Walk Contents

The existing Walk Contents screen (currently called Previous Days) belongs to an individual Walk only.

It should never be opened directly from a Journey.

It should only be accessible after selecting a specific Walk.

---

## Dynamic Behaviour

Generate the Walk list from the Journey.

Do not hard-code:

Who Is God

Created in God's Image

etc.

Any number of Walks should be supported.

---

## Design

Do not redesign the interface.

Reuse the existing card style.

The Journey Details page should feel like a natural extension of the current design.

---

## Goal

The navigation hierarchy must always be:

Next Steps

↓

Journeys

↓

Journey

↓

Walk

↓

Walk Introduction

↓

Steps

↓

Walk Complete

This architecture must support hundreds of Walks without becoming confusing.
