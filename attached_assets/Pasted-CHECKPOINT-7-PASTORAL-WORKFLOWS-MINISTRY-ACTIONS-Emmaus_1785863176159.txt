CHECKPOINT 7 — PASTORAL WORKFLOWS & MINISTRY ACTIONS

Emmaus now understands people, discipleship, attendance, care signals and church health.

The next step is helping pastors and ministry leaders respond.

This checkpoint is NOT about sending notifications.

It is about turning Care Signals into real pastoral care.

The goal is to help churches ensure that no one is overlooked.

----------------------------------------------------
DESIGN PRINCIPLES
----------------------------------------------------

Emmaus must never replace pastoral care.

Emmaus should simply help leaders:

• notice
• assign
• remember
• follow through

Every workflow should lead to a real conversation, visit, prayer or act of care.

----------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1
