# Emmaus Content Studio - Journey Complete Editor

## Objective

Journey Complete is not a normal journey step.

It marks the completion of an entire journey, regardless of whether that journey contains 5 days, 14 days, 30 days or any other number of steps.

It should therefore have its own dedicated editor.

---

## Current Problem

Journey Complete currently uses the same editor as a normal journey step.

It includes fields such as:

- Welcome
- Scripture
- Reflection
- Prayer
- Today's Step
- Looking Ahead

These fields belong to the daily content of a journey and are not appropriate for the completion page.

---

## Required Behaviour

Create a dedicated Journey Complete editor with the following fields:

### Title

(Default: Journey Complete)

---

### Congratulations

A rich text field summarising the journey, celebrating what God has been teaching the user and encouraging them to continue walking with Jesus.

---

### Closing Prayer

A prayer thanking God for what He has done during the journey and asking Him to continue His work.

---

### Recommended Next Journey

Journey picker.

Allow the author to select the next recommended journey from the Journey Library.

Display in the app as:

Continue to [Journey Name] →

---

### Completion Message (Optional)

A short closing message displayed after the user completes the journey.

Example:

"Well done. Keep walking with Jesus."

---

## Remove These Fields

Journey Complete should not contain:

- Scripture
- Reflection
- Today's Step
- Looking Ahead

These belong only to the daily journey steps.

---

## Design Principle

Journey Introduction, Daily Steps and Journey Complete are three different content types.

Each should have its own editor that matches its purpose.

Journey Complete should always feel like the conclusion of one chapter and the natural beginning of the next, regardless of how many steps the journey contains.
