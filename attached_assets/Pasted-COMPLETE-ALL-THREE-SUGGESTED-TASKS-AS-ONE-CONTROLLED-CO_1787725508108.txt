==================================================
1. FIX ACCIDENTAL BIBLE NAVIGATION
==================================================

A Bible reference must not itself trigger navigation.

Classify the user’s intention before executing any Bible action.

Required distinctions:

“What does John 3:16 mean?”

- mode: ASK
- remain in Ask Emmaus
- answer the question
- display a clickable John 3:16 reference
- do not navigate

“Read John 3:16.”

- mode: READ
- open or expand the Read to Me player
- read the verse
- preserve the current page where possible
- do not navigate to an unrelated Bible page

“Open John 3:16.”

- mode: OPEN
- navigate to John 3
- scroll to verse 16
- highlight verse 16

“Find a Bible Study on John 3:16.”

- mode: FIND
- search eligible Bible Studies
- show results inside Ask Emmaus
- do not navigate until requested

Navigation is permitted only for an explicit OPEN action or another action whose validated definition specifically permits navigation.

Remove any fallback that opens:

- the beginning of the Bible book
- My Bible home
- a generic Bible page
- an unrelated passage

If a precise destination cannot be validated, remain in Ask Emmaus.

Add tests for Bible references appearing in:

- questions
- explanations
- sermon searches
- Walk searches
- follow-up messages
- reading requests
- opening requests
