Project Emmaus — Fix App Launch Routing for 10 Minutes with Jesus

This is a critical member-flow bug.

Current incorrect behaviour:

Emmaus can open into a screen that says:

“You’re ahead of the rhythm.
Today’s 10 Minutes with Jesus will be ready soon.
Check back later.”

This must never happen during normal app launch.

====================================================
LOCKED DAILY ENTRY FLOW
====================================================

Emmaus must behave like this every day:

FIRST OPEN OF THE DAY

If today’s 10 Minutes with Jesus has not been completed:

Open directly into today’s available 10 Minutes with Jesus entry.

Do not open Today’s Steps first.

Do not open a locked-future-day screen.

Do not show “You’re ahead of the rhythm.”

AFTER COMPLETING TODAY’S 10 MINUTES WITH JESUS

Immediately navigate to:

Today’s Steps

Do not advance to tomorrow’s entry.

Do not show a completion dead end.

Do not require the member to press Back to Today’s Steps.

EVERY SUBSEQUENT OPENING ON THE SAME DAY

Open directly to:

Today’s Steps

Do not reopen 10 Minutes with Jesus automatically.

Do not show the future-day lock screen.

====================================================
CORE RULE
====================================================

The app-entry decision must be based on:

1. member local date;
2. today’s available Daily Rhythm day;
3. whether that exact day is completed;
4. whether the user is in authorised Development Mode.

It must not be based only on:

- last viewed day;
- highest completed day;
- currentDay + 1;
- last route;
- stale local storage;
- browser history;
- the next chronological entry.

====================================================
TASK 1 — CREATE ONE CANONICAL APP-ENTRY RESOLVER
====================================================

Create or reuse one shared function such as:

resolveEmmausEntryRoute({
  memberId,
  localDate,
  developmentMode
})

It should return exactly one of:

- today’s Daily Rhythm route;
- Today’s Steps route;
- onboarding route where genuinely required.

Do not let multiple screens independently decide where the app opens.

All launch paths must use this resolver:

- fresh app open;
- browser refresh;
- sign-in redirect;
- splash completion;
- root route;
- returning from an expired session;
- installed PWA launch;
- mobile browser launch.

====================================================
TASK 2 — CALCULATE TODAY’S AVAILABLE RHYTHM DAY
====================================================

Use the established Daily Rhythm calendar logic.

Determine the member’s available day from:

- Daily Rhythm start date;
- member start date where applicable;
- member local calendar date;
- published entries;
- Development Mode.

Do not calculate today’s route as:

lastCompletedDay + 1

unless the calendar confirms that day is currently available.

Do not route to a day beyond today’s available day.

====================================================
TASK 3 — COMPLETION CHECK
====================================================

Check whether the member has completed today’s exact Daily Rhythm entry.

Example:

todayAvailableDay = 5

If Day 5 is incomplete:

route to Day 5.

If Day 5 is complete:

route to Today’s Steps.

Do not route to Day 6 merely because Day 5 is complete.

Day 6 remains unavailable until the next eligible local calendar day.

====================================================
TASK 4 — AFTER COMPLETION
====================================================

When the member taps the completion action on today’s 10 Minutes with Jesus:

1. save completion for today’s entry;
2. wait for successful server confirmation;
3. invalidate Daily Rhythm progress queries;
4. navigate with replace semantics to Today’s Steps;
5. prevent browser Back from returning to the just-completed active flow where possible.

Use:

Today’s Steps

as the canonical destination.

Do not navigate to:

- next day;
- Previous Days;
- future-day lock screen;
- Daily Rhythm series page;
- generic completion screen.

====================================================
TASK 5 — SAME-DAY REOPENING
====================================================

After today’s entry is complete, every same-day app launch must open Today’s Steps.

Verify this for:

- closing and reopening the browser;
- refreshing the root route;
- signing out and back in;
- reopening the installed app;
- opening the normal Emmaus URL;
- returning from another tab.

Do not rely only on in-memory state.

The completion state must come from persisted member progress.

====================================================
TASK 6 — FUTURE-DAY LOCK SCREEN
====================================================

Keep the “You’re ahead of the rhythm” screen only as a defensive route guard.

It may appear only when a member explicitly or accidentally attempts to open a Daily Rhythm day later than today’s available day.

Examples:

- manually entering a future day URL;
- stale browser bookmark;
- invalid internal route;
- Development Mode disabled after viewing future content.

It must never be used as:

- the root app route;
- splash destination;
- normal post-completion destination;
- same-day reopen destination.

====================================================
TASK 7 — FUTURE-DAY GUARD DESTINATION
====================================================

When a normal member attempts to open a future Daily Rhythm day, prefer redirecting them to Today’s Steps instead of leaving them on the “ahead of the rhythm” screen.

Recommended behaviour:

Future-day route detected
→ redirect to Today’s Steps
→ optionally show a subtle message:

“Today’s time with Jesus is complete. The next day will be ready tomorrow.”

Do not create a full-screen dead end during normal use.

The existing “View Previous Days” access should remain available from Today’s Steps.

====================================================
TASK 8 — SPLASH FLOW
====================================================

Preserve the locked splash experience:

Emmaus

Walk with Jesus

After the splash, call the canonical app-entry resolver.

Do not hard-code the splash to open Daily Rhythm unconditionally.

Do not hard-code it to open Today’s Steps unconditionally.

The resolver decides:

- today incomplete → today’s 10 Minutes with Jesus;
- today complete → Today’s Steps.

====================================================
TASK 9 — DEVELOPMENT MODE
====================================================

Development Mode may allow authorised testers to open future published days manually.

However:

- Development Mode must not alter production-member launch logic;
- normal app launch should still use today’s relevant state unless the tester deliberately selected another day;
- finishing a test day must not corrupt the real calendar schedule;
- switching Development Mode off must return to the normal resolver.

Do not let a Development Mode route persist as the default production launch route.

====================================================
TASK 10 — CLEAR STALE ROUTE STATE
====================================================

Inspect and remove any launch logic using stale values such as:

- lastDailyRhythmRoute;
- currentDay stored in localStorage;
- lastViewedDay;
- nextDay;
- resumePath;
- browser history state;
- cached selected day.

These values may support editor/testing navigation, but they must not override the canonical launch resolver.

On completion, clear any stale stored route that points to a future Daily Rhythm day.

====================================================
TASK 11 — ROOT AND ALIAS ROUTES
====================================================

Inspect every member entry route, including likely routes such as:

/
 /walk
 /app
 /today
 /daily-rhythm
 authenticated redirect
 splash callback

Ensure they all resolve through the same entry logic.

Do not allow one route to use the correct logic while another still opens the future-day screen.

====================================================
TASK 12 — LOADING STATE
====================================================

While the app-entry resolver is checking:

- member session;
- local date;
- published Daily Rhythm entry;
- today’s completion state;

show a neutral Emmaus loading state or splash continuation.

Do not briefly render the future-day screen and then redirect.

Do not flash Today’s Steps before opening today’s reading.

Wait until the route decision is known.

====================================================
TASK 13 — TIMEZONE
====================================================

Use the member’s local calendar date.

For Pastor Jeremy’s current testing environment, this should respect South African local time.

Do not use UTC midnight as the member’s day boundary.

Test around:

- 11:59 PM local time;
- 12:00 AM local time;
- timezone offset changes;
- server and browser date differences.

====================================================
TASK 14 — FAILURE SAFETY
====================================================

If progress cannot be loaded:

- do not guess the next day;
- do not route to a future entry;
- do not display raw errors.

Use a safe fallback:

Today’s Steps

with a clean retry mechanism where necessary.

Log the real error server-side.

====================================================

Use the current member and Daily Rhythm records.

SCENARIO A — FIRST OPEN, DAY INCOMPLETE

✓ No future-day screen appears.

✓ Today’s Steps does not appear first.

✓ App navigates directly to Today’s Steps.

✓ It does not show “You’re ahead of the rhythm.”

SCENARIO E — FUTURE URL

✓ Manually open a future Daily Rhythm URL.

✓ Member is redirected safely to Today’s Steps.

SCENARIO F — DEVELOPMENT MODE

✓ Tester can deliberately open a future published day.

✓ Normal-member launch behaviour remains correct.

✓ Turning Development Mode off restores normal routing.

====================================================

====================================================

Do not redesign the “You’re ahead of the rhythm” screen as the solution.

Do not change its wording and leave the launch bug intact.

Do not merely redirect one visible route.

Find every normal app-entry path and route all of them through one canonical resolver.

The locked rule is:

Before today’s 10 Minutes with Jesus is complete:
Emmaus opens today’s reading.

After it is complete:
Emmaus opens Today’s Steps for the rest of that local day.

Under no normal circumstance may Emmaus open into the future-day lock screen.
