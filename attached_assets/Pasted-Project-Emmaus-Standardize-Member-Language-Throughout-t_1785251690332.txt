Project Emmaus — Standardize Member Language Throughout the App

We are establishing a permanent language principle for Emmaus.

Emmaus is not a productivity app.

Emmaus is not a habit tracker.

Emmaus is a gentle companion that invites people to spend time with Jesus.

Every piece of language should reflect this philosophy.

==================================================
LANGUAGE PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Avoid command-driven wording.

Avoid instructional wording.

Avoid productivity language.

Avoid wording that makes discipleship feel like completing tasks.

Emmaus should always sound warm, invitational and pastoral.

The app opens content.

Jesus changes lives.

==================================================
BUTTON STANDARD
==================================================

Primary buttons should open content rather than instruct behaviour.

Replace wording as follows wherever appropriate.

Begin Devotional
→ Open Devotional

Begin Journey
→ Open Journey

Begin Sermon Companion
→ Open Companion

Begin Day 1
→ Open Today's Time

Continue Day
→ Continue

Resume Journey
→ Continue Journey

Review Today
→ Review

Review Journey
→ Review Journey

Only use "Continue" when progress already exists.

Only use "Review" once content has been completed.

==================================================
CARD HEADINGS
==================================================

Avoid wording such as

Begin your daily walk

Start your journey

Complete today's lesson

Instead use gentle invitations.

Examples:

Today's time with Jesus is ready.

Spend a few moments with Jesus today.

Continue where you left off.

Your devotional is ready.

This week's sermon companion is ready.

Continue walking with Jesus.

These are examples only.

The principle matters more than the exact wording.

==================================================
IMPORTANT
==================================================

Do not change navigation.

Do not change progress logic.

Do not change completion logic.

Only update member-facing wording.

==================================================
SPECIAL CASE
==================================================

Daily Rhythm is compulsory.

Members should never see

"Begin your daily walk"

because opening Emmaus already means they have begun.

Instead the card should simply invite them into today's time with Jesus.

==================================================
SUCCESS CRITERIA
==================================================

✓ Emmaus sounds like a caring mentor.

✓ Buttons open content instead of commanding behaviour.

✓ No productivity language.

✓ No checklist language.

✓ The tone is consistently gentle, invitational and pastoral across the entire member experience.
