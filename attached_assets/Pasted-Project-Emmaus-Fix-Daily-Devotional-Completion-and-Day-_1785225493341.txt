Project Emmaus — Fix Daily Devotional Completion and Day Advancement

Current problem:

In the member Daily Devotional reading screen, pressing Continue at the end of Day 1 immediately advances the member to Day 2.

This is incorrect.

Daily Devotionals are calendar-based daily content.

A normal member must not move directly from Day 1 to Day 2 on the same day.

Development Mode may bypass the daily lock for authorised testers.

Fix this specific behaviour completely.

Do not redesign the Daily Devotional architecture.
Do not merge Daily Devotional progress with Journey progress.
Do not modify Daily Rhythm.
Do not generate new content.

====================================================
LOCKED EXPECTED BEHAVIOUR
====================================================

At the end of a Daily Devotional entry, the primary button should be:

Finished

Do not use “Continue” when it implies opening the next day.

When a normal member taps Finished:

1. Mark the current Daily Devotional entry as completed/read.
2. Save completion for the current member.
3. Do not advance currentDay to the next entry unless that next entry is already unlocked by the calendar.
4. Do not navigate to Day 2.
5. Return the member to Today’s Steps.
6. Keep the Daily Devotional card visible with the correct completed state for today.

Day 2 should only become available on the next eligible local calendar day.

Development Mode may allow the tester to open Day 2 manually.

====================================================
TASK 1 — CHANGE THE END BUTTON
====================================================

On the live Daily Devotional reading screen:

Replace:

Continue

with:

Finished

The button should clearly mean:

“I have finished today’s devotional.”

It must not mean:

“Open the next day.”

Use the existing Emmaus primary button style.

Do not add confetti, badges, XP, celebration screens or streak pressure.

====================================================
TASK 2 — SEPARATE COMPLETION FROM ADVANCEMENT
====================================================

Inspect the current completion handler.

The current logic appears to do something like:

complete current entry
→ increment currentDay
→ navigate to next entry

Replace this with two separate concepts:

A. completion
B. availability/unlock

Completion records that the member finished the current entry.

Availability is calculated from:

- series start date;
- member start date;
- local calendar date;
- publication status;
- Development Mode.

Tapping Finished must only perform completion.

It must not automatically increment the member to the next locked day.

====================================================
TASK 3 — MEMBER PROGRESS DATA
====================================================

Use the existing separate Daily Devotional progress record.

Ensure it can store:

- memberId
- seriesId
- startedAt
- startDateLocal
- lastCompletedDay
- completedEntryIds or completedDays
- lastReadAt
- currentAvailableDay, if stored
- updatedAt

Prefer calculating currentAvailableDay from dates rather than blindly incrementing it on button press.

Do not use Journey progress.

Do not use Daily Rhythm progress.

Prevent duplicate completion records when Finished is tapped more than once.

====================================================
TASK 4 — CALENDAR UNLOCK RULE
====================================================

For normal members:

Day 1 is available on the day the member begins the devotional series.

Day 2 becomes available on the next local calendar day.

Day 3 becomes available on the following local calendar day.

Use the member’s local date, not only UTC elapsed hours.

Example:

Member begins Day 1 at 10:00 PM.

Day 2 may unlock after local midnight.

The member should not be forced to wait a full 24 hours.

Suggested calculation:

availableDay =
daysBetween(memberStartLocalDate, memberCurrentLocalDate) + 1

Clamp the value to:

- minimum 1;
- maximum number of published entries.

Only published entries may unlock.

Do not unlock a missing or draft entry.

====================================================
TASK 5 — AFTER FINISHED IS TAPPED
====================================================

After successful completion:

Navigate to:

Today’s Steps

Do not navigate to:

- Day 2;
- Previous Days;
- series details;
- a completion screen.

Show a subtle success state if the project already has one, such as:

“Today’s devotional is complete.”

Keep it brief and non-intrusive.

No modal is required.
