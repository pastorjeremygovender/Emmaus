IMPORTANT: READ THIS ENTIRE PROMPT BEFORE MAKING CHANGES.

Build Phase 1 of the Emmaus Discipleship and Pastoral Care Dashboard inside the EXISTING Emmaus Admin system.

This is not a separate app, not a Wix build, and not a replacement for the existing admin panel.

The purpose is to help pastors and authorised leaders see:

- who is attending church;
- who is actively engaging with Emmaus;
- who is progressing through Walks and Journeys;
- who may need encouragement or pastoral follow-up;
- who may be ready for baptism, serving or another discipleship step.

This must be a pastoral-care tool, not a surveillance system or spiritual scoring system.

==================================================
CORE PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Attendance is one part of discipleship.

Emmaus must combine:

- church attendance;
- Walk and Journey activity;
- Emmaus engagement;
- pastoral follow-up;
- serving preparation;
- positive discipleship milestones.

Do not create:

- spiritual scores;
- public rankings;
- labels such as faithful, unfaithful or backslidden;
- automatic reprimands;
- automatic messages to absent members;
- conclusions about someone’s relationship with Jesus.

Emmaus identifies patterns.

A human pastor or leader decides whether and how to respond.

==================================================
1. DISCIPLESHIP DASHBOARD
==================================================

Create a new dashboard focused on pastoral awareness.

The dashboard should answer:

- Who may need attention?
- Who is actively growing?
- Who has completed an important next step?
- Who has stopped attending or engaging unexpectedly?
- What follow-ups are still outstanding?

Create the following dashboard sections.

--------------------------------------------------
A. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------

Show simple summary cards:

- Attendance at latest Sunday meeting
- First-time visitors
- People currently active in Walks
- Walks completed this week
- People needing follow-up
- Follow-ups overdue
- People with recent positive milestones

Do not overcrowd the dashboard.

Each summary card should open the relevant filtered list.

--------------------------------------------------
B. NEEDS ATTENTION
--------------------------------------------------

Display rule-based care signals such as:

“Jeremy has missed 3 consecutive Sunday services.”

“Sarah was regularly active in Emmaus but has not engaged for 14 days.”

“David stopped attending and also stopped progressing through his active Walk.”

“Michael began the Volunteer Walk but has not continued recently.”

“Thandi missed two Made Free meetings.”

Each card must show:

- person’s name;
- reason for the signal;
- date the signal was created;
- priority;
- assigned leader, if any;
- current follow-up status.

Actions:

- View Person
- Assign Leader
- Add Note
- Mark Contacted
- Snooze
- Mark Resolved

Do not automatically contact the person.

--------------------------------------------------
C. POSITIVE NEXT STEPS
--------------------------------------------------

Show positive pastoral opportunities such as:

“Jason completed the Volunteer Walk.”

“Sarah completed Beginning Your New Life.”

“David completed the Baptism Journey.”

“Nomsa has attended regularly since joining.”

“Peter completed Core Foundations.”

Actions may include:

- View Person
- Assign Follow-up
- Mark Ready for Serving
- Mark Ready for Baptism Conversation
- Recommend Next Walk
- Dismiss

These are suggestions, not automatic decisions.

--------------------------------------------------
D. RECENT ACTIVITY
--------------------------------------------------

Show a compact timeline of meaningful events:

- attended a meeting;
- started a Walk;
- completed a Step;
- completed a Walk;
- joined a Room;
- received a pastoral follow-up;
- marked ready to serve;
- baptism status updated.

Do not include every page view or meaningless click.

6. CARE SIGNAL RULES
==================================================

Phase 1 must use transparent, editable rules.

Do not use predictive AI yet.

Create Care Rules under:

Settings → Discipleship and Care Rules

Initial rules:

--------------------------------------------------
A. MISSED ATTENDANCE
--------------------------------------------------

Create a signal when someone misses 3 consecutive sessions of a meeting they normally attend.

Do not count:

- Not Expected;
- Apology;
- cancelled meetings;
- meetings before the person began attending.

Suggested message:

“Jeremy has missed 3 consecutive Sunday Morning services.”

--------------------------------------------------
B. CHANGE FROM NORMAL PATTERN
--------------------------------------------------

Create a signal when someone who normally attends regularly suddenly stops.

The system should consider their personal attendance pattern, not only a universal threshold.

Suggested message:

“Sarah usually attends Sunday Morning but has missed the last 3 services.”

--------------------------------------------------
C. EMMAUS DISENGAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------

Create a signal when someone who was recently active in Emmaus has no meaningful engagement for 14 days.

Meaningful engagement includes:

- Daily Rhythm;
- devotional completion;
- Walk Step completion;
- Bible Study progress;
- Sermon Companion progress;
- Room participation where appropriate.

Do not create this signal for a person who has never used Emmaus meaningfully.

--------------------------------------------------
D. COMBINED DISENGAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------

Create a higher-priority signal when:

- church attendance has stopped; and
- Emmaus engagement has also stopped.

Suggested message:

“David has recently stopped attending and has also stopped engaging with Emmaus.”

--------------------------------------------------
E. WALK INTERRUPTION
--------------------------------------------------

Create a signal when someone was progressing through a Walk and then stops for a configurable period.

Do not trigger this for every casual or newly started Walk.

Suggested message:

“Michael was progressing through the Volunteer Walk but has not continued recently.”

--------------------------------------------------
F. POSITIVE WALK COMPLETION
--------------------------------------------------

Create a positive signal when someone completes a selected Walk.

Examples:

- Volunteer Walk
- Baptism Journey
- Beginning Your New Life
- Core Foundations
- Made Free

Suggested message:

“Jason completed the Volunteer Walk. Consider a serving conversation.”

--------------------------------------------------
G. SENSITIVE WALK RULES
--------------------------------------------------

Sensitive programmes such as Made Free must use restricted visibility.

Only explicitly authorised leaders may see:

- participation;
- progress;
- attendance;
- care signals;
- notes.

General administrators must not automatically have access.
