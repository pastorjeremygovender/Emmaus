Fix the Emmaus Group role and permission system.

CONFIRMED BUG

The “Emmaus Test” Group was created by ICC. ICC is correctly displayed with the crown as the Group Owner/Leader.

Jeremy joined the Group as an ordinary member and has no crown or Leader designation. However, Jeremy can currently:

- Add or edit the Leader’s Note
- Add a Walk
- Schedule a meeting
- Start a meeting

This is incorrect. An ordinary Group member must not receive Leader permissions because they are an Emmaus administrator, church administrator, content administrator, or Pastor elsewhere in the application.

Group permissions must be determined by the user’s role inside that specific Group.

REQUIRED GROUP ROLES

Implement or confirm these three Group-specific roles:

1. OWNER

The person or church account that created the Group.

The Owner may:

- Edit Group details and settings
- Add, change, or remove a Walk, Journey, or other Group content
- Add or edit the Leader’s Note
- Schedule, edit, or cancel meetings
- Start and end meetings
- Invite and remove members
- Promote a member to Leader
- Remove a Leader
- Transfer ownership
- Archive or delete the Group

There must be only one Owner unless the existing architecture intentionally supports organisational ownership.

2. LEADER

A member explicitly appointed by the Owner or another authorized Leader.

A Leader may:

- Add, change, or remove Group content
- Add or edit the Leader’s Note
- Schedule, edit, or cancel meetings
- Start and end meetings
- Invite members
- Use Group Leader tools
- Manage members only where specifically permitted

A Leader must be visibly marked as “Leader” in the member list. Use a suitable Leader badge or icon. Reserve the crown for the Owner.

3. MEMBER

A normal person who joins through an invitation or access code.

A Member may:

- View the Group
- Read the Leader’s Note
- View the selected Walk or Journey
- View scheduled meeting details
- Join a meeting after an Owner or Leader has started it
- Participate in discussions and other permitted Group activities
- Complete assigned content
- Leave the Group

A Member must not be able to:

- Add, change, or remove a Walk, Journey, or other Group content
- Add, edit, or delete the Leader’s Note
- Schedule, edit, or cancel a meeting
- Start or end a meeting
- Access Leader meeting controls
- Change Group settings
- Promote, demote, invite, or remove members unless specifically authorized
- Archive or delete the Group

IMPORTANT PERMISSION RULE

Global application roles and Group roles must remain separate.

Being any of the following must not automatically grant leadership control inside every Group:

- Emmaus administrator
- ICC administrator
- Church administrator
- Content administrator
- Pastor
- Staff member
- Group creator in another Group

A user receives Group leadership permissions only when that user is the Owner or has explicitly been assigned the Leader role in that particular Group.

Jeremy must remain an ordinary Member in the “Emmaus Test” Group unless ICC explicitly promotes Jeremy to Leader.

USER INTERFACE FIX

For an ordinary Member such as Jeremy, change the Group screen as follows:

GROUP CONTENT

- Display the selected Walk or Journey if one has been added.
- If no content is selected, show:
  “No Walk or Journey has been added yet.”
- Do not show “Add a Walk.”
- Do not make the empty content area tappable.

LEADER’S NOTE

- If a note exists, show it as read-only.
- Do not show “Add note,” an edit icon, or any editable field.
- If no note exists, either hide the section or show:
  “No Leader’s Note has been added yet.”
- Do not show the editable placeholder:
  “Add a note for your group…”

MEETING

- Display scheduled meeting information as read-only.
- Do not show “Schedule” or “Schedule a meeting.”
- If no meeting is scheduled, show:
  “No meeting has been scheduled yet.”

START/JOIN BUTTON

- Do not show “Start Meeting” to Members.
- Before a Leader starts the meeting, either show no button or a disabled status:
  “Meeting has not started.”
- Once the Owner or a Leader starts the meeting, show:
  “Join Meeting”
- A Member tapping “Join Meeting” should enter the active meeting as a participant, never as a Leader or host.

MEMBER LIST

- Display the Owner with a crown and the label “Owner.”
- Display appointed Leaders with a clear “Leader” badge.
- Display ordinary users without a crown or Leader badge.
- Continue showing “(you)” next to the current user.

OWNER LEADERSHIP MANAGEMENT

Add a safe way for the Owner to appoint a Member as Leader:

- Open the Member’s menu
- Select “Make Leader”
- Show a confirmation:
  “Make [name] a Leader of this Group?”
- On confirmation, update that member’s Group-specific role to Leader

Allow the Owner to select:

- “Remove as Leader”

This must return the person to the Member role without removing them from the Group.

Do not allow an ordinary Member to promote themselves by manipulating the interface or API request.

SERVER-SIDE SECURITY

Do not fix this only by hiding buttons.

Every protected Group action must check the authenticated user’s role for that exact Group on the server before making any change.

Owner or Leader authorization is required for endpoints/actions that:

- Add, change, or remove Group content
- Create, edit, or delete a Leader’s Note
- Schedule, update, or cancel a meeting
- Start or end a meeting
- Access host/Leader meeting credentials
- Manage invitations where restricted
- Promote, demote, or remove members
- Change Group settings

Owner-only authorization is required for:

- Promoting or demoting Leaders, unless intentionally delegated
- Transferring ownership
- Archiving or deleting the Group

Unauthorized requests must return HTTP 403 with a safe message such as:

“You do not have permission to perform this Group action.”

Do not rely on role information supplied by the client. Load the current user’s Group membership and role from the database for every protected mutation.

LIVE MEETING SECURITY

Meeting credentials or LiveKit tokens must reflect the Group role:

- Owner/Leader: host or moderator permissions
- Member: participant permissions only

A Member must not be able to obtain a host token by directly calling the meeting-start or token endpoint.

DATA AND MIGRATION SAFETY

Do not delete or recreate existing Groups.

Preserve:

- Existing Groups
- Owners
- Leaders
- Members
- Invitations and access codes
- Selected content
- Leader’s Notes
- Scheduled meetings
- Discussions and messages
- Existing meeting records

Inspect the existing schema before changing it.

If Group roles are missing or inconsistent, safely migrate existing records:

- Existing Group creator becomes Owner
- Existing explicitly appointed Group Leaders remain Leaders
- Everyone else becomes Member
- Do not promote all administrators or staff to Group Leaders

If a Group was created by an organisation account such as ICC, preserve ICC as Owner.

REACTIVE PERMISSION STATE

After a user is promoted or demoted, refresh the Group permissions immediately.

Do not require the user to sign out and sign in again.

Ensure permission queries use the current Group ID and current authenticated user. Do not reuse cached leadership permissions from another Group.
