# CHECKPOINT 4 – Care Signals Engine

The Discipleship Profile is now complete.

The next feature is the Care Signals Engine.

This should become one of Emmaus' flagship features.

The goal is NOT to analyse people.

The goal is simply to notice meaningful discipleship changes that a pastor may otherwise miss.

Emmaus must never diagnose motives or emotions.

It only observes objective events and meaningful changes.

Every signal must be explainable by data.

Every signal must contain the exact evidence that triggered it.

No AI guesses.

No psychological conclusions.

No hidden scoring.

No "risk rating."

Only observations.

---

## Design Principles

Signals should feel like another caring leader quietly saying,

"I noticed something you may want to know."

Never

"This person has a problem."

---

## Categories

Create five signal categories.

🟢 Celebration

🔵 Growth

🟡 Attention

🟠 Follow-up

🔴 Significant

Each signal belongs to one category.

---

## Initial Signal Library

### Celebration

Completed first Walk

Completed Made Free

Completed Foundations

Finished Bible Study

Joined first Room

Started serving

100-day Daily Rhythm streak

Baptised

Accepted Christ

Prayer request marked answered

---

### Growth

Started a new Walk

Started Daily Rhythm

Joined a Room

Started Bible Study

Started serving

Completed another milestone

Consistent attendance for 8 weeks

Regular Emmaus engagement

---

### Attention

Attendance declining compared with previous pattern

Daily Rhythm stopped

Walk inactive for 14 days

Bible Study inactive

Room participation stopped

Serving paused

Prayer request open for more than 30 days

---

### Follow-up

Missed three expected services

No Emmaus activity for 30 days

Stopped Made Free midway

Stopped Foundations midway

Left every Room

Removed all active Walks

Repeated attendance corrections

---

### Significant

Accepted Christ

Baptised

Hospital visit recorded

Bereavement recorded

Marriage

Birth of child

Pastoral intervention recorded

Major life milestone

These should stay visible longer than ordinary signals.

Status

New

Assigned leader

Optional pastoral note

Every status change must be audited.

---

## Explainability

Every signal must include exactly why it appeared.

Example

Attendance Declining

Reason

Attended 11 of previous 12 services.

Attended 2 of last 6 services.

Detected today.

NOT

"Jeremy appears to be disengaging."

Never infer.

Only report facts.

## Notifications

Do NOT send notifications automatically.

Only surface signals inside the Admin Dashboard.

Future checkpoints may enable notifications.

---

## Rules Engine

Build every signal as an independent rule.

Each rule should have

Name

Enabled

Threshold

Severity

Description

Evidence builder

Rules should be configurable later from Admin Settings.

Adding a new signal should require creating one new rule only.

Existing rules should never need modification.

---

## Success Criteria

When a pastor opens Emmaus on Monday morning they should immediately see

Who should be celebrated

Who is growing

Who may need encouragement

Who needs follow-up

Why Emmaus generated each signal

The dashboard should save pastors time while keeping discipleship—not administration—at the centre.

No assumptions.

No AI diagnoses.

Only clear, explainable observations supported by objective data.
