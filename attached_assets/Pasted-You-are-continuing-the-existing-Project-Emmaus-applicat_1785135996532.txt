You are continuing the existing Project Emmaus application.

Do not redesign the Daily Rhythm experience.

Do not change navigation.

Do not change content.

This sprint is typography and reading experience only.

=====================================================

OBJECTIVE

Create a clean, calm, distraction-free reading experience.

Emmaus should feel like quietly reading Scripture and reflecting with Jesus.

Not like reading a word processor.

=====================================================

TYPOGRAPHY

Use ONE font family throughout the Daily Rhythm experience.

Do not mix serif and sans-serif fonts.

Do not switch fonts for prayers.

Do not use cursive fonts.

Do not use decorative fonts.

Use the same font family everywhere.

Only vary:

- font size
- weight
- spacing

=====================================================

HIERARCHY

10 Minutes with Jesus

Largest text.

Bold.

Centered.

-----------------------------------------------------

Day Number

Smaller than title.

Medium weight.

Centered.

-----------------------------------------------------

Day Title

Example:

Come and See

Large.

Bold.

Centered.

-----------------------------------------------------

Section Headings

Use consistent styling.

Examples:

SCRIPTURE

REFLECTION

PRAYER

YOUR NEXT STEP

Small uppercase.

Semibold.

Consistent spacing.

Do not use different heading styles.

-----------------------------------------------------

Body Text

Use the same font everywhere.

Regular weight.

Comfortable line spacing.

Easy to read on mobile.

No justified text.

=====================================================

ALIGNMENT

Center only:

- 10 Minutes with Jesus
- Day Number
- Day Title

Everything else should be LEFT aligned.

This includes:

Greeting

Reflection

Prayer

Next Step

Closing

Reading paragraphs should never be centered.

=====================================================

PRAYER

Prayer should use the EXACT SAME FONT as the rest of the body.

Do not italicise the entire prayer.

Do not change font family.

Do not change typography.

Only the heading "PRAYER" distinguishes the section.

=====================================================

SCRIPTURE

Scripture reference:

John 1:35–39

Use body font.

Do not over-style it.

The dynamically loaded Bible passage should inherit the same body typography.

=====================================================

SPACING

Increase whitespace between sections.

Do not crowd paragraphs.

Keep a calm rhythm.

Allow each paragraph to breathe.

=====================================================

WIDTH

Limit reading width for desktop preview.

Long lines become difficult to read.

Use a comfortable maximum content width while remaining fully responsive on mobile.

=====================================================

COLOURS

Keep colours minimal.

Primary text:

Dark charcoal.

Secondary labels:

Muted grey.

No decorative colours.

=====================================================

DO NOT

Do not add cards.

Do not add gradients.

Do not add icons.

Do not add animations.

Do not add decorative dividers.

Do not add badges.

Do not add illustrations.

The content should remain the focus.
