# EMMAUS ROOMS — FULL INTERACTIVE LEADER EXPERIENCE

We are proceeding with the complete interactive Rooms experience.

Do NOT reduce scope to video only.

The goal is to make an Emmaus Room feel like a live shared discipleship space where a leader can guide the group through Scripture, content, discussion, prayer and interaction in real time.

Preserve all existing:
- LiveKit integration
- room permissions
- room membership
- room invitations
- current Room UI
- discussion
- prayer requests
- shared progress
- Ask Emmaus
- database and APIs unless additions are required

==================================================
CORE EXPERIENCE
==================================================

An authorized Room Leader should be able to guide everyone in the Room through the same discipleship experience in real time.

Build:

1. Gather Together
2. Shared Scripture
3. Leader-controlled navigation
4. Shared highlighting
5. Shared notes
6. Shared Ask Emmaus
7. Polls
8. Prayer Mode
9. Attendance
10. Session completion

All of these should feel like parts of ONE Room, not separate apps or pages.

==================================================
AUTHORIZED LEADER CONTROLS
==================================================

For Authorized Group Leaders, Pastors and Super Admins, add:

GUIDE GROUP

• Open Today's Step
• Open Scripture for Everyone
• Go to Previous Step
• Go to Next Step
• Start Discussion
• Prayer Time
• Launch Poll
• Ask Emmaus Together
• Gather Together
• End Gathering
• Complete Session

Do not expose these controls to ordinary members.

Keep them accessible through a compact "Guide Group" control rather than cluttering the Room.

==================================================
1. GATHER TOGETHER
==================================================

Use the existing LiveKit integration.

Leader:
- Start Gathering
- End Gathering

Members:
- Join Gathering
- Leave Gathering

Embedded video remains inside the Room.

Do not navigate users away from the Room.

==================================================
2. SHARED SCRIPTURE
==================================================

Authorized Leader can tap:

Open Scripture for Everyone

Choose:
- Bible book
- chapter
- optional verse range

When selected:

Every connected Room member should receive the navigation event and open the same Scripture passage.

The member should not have to manually navigate.

Display a subtle notice:

"Pastor Jeremy opened John 15."

Allow members to browse independently afterward unless "Follow Leader" is enabled.

==================================================
FOLLOW LEADER
==================================================

Add a simple toggle for members:

Follow Leader

Default ON during a live guided session.

When ON:
- Leader navigation changes move the member to the same Step/Scripture/panel.

When OFF:
- Member can browse independently.

The user should always be able to re-enable Follow Leader.

==================================================
3. SHARED HIGHLIGHTING
==================================================

Allow Room participants to highlight Scripture during the Room session.

Store:

- roomId
- scripture reference
- verse
- userId
- highlight
- optional note
- timestamp

Highlights should be visible to everyone in the Room.

Show attribution subtly:

Jeremy
Sarah
John

Do not use competitive colors or rankings.

Tapping a shared highlight can show:

"Jeremy highlighted this verse."

Optional:

Why this stood out...

==================================================
LEADER HIGHLIGHT
==================================================

Authorized Leader can mark a verse as:

Focus Verse

When selected:

- all followers are moved to the verse
- verse receives a clear but calm emphasis
- members see:

"Focus Verse"

This should not overwrite personal highlights.

==================================================
4. SHARED NOTES
==================================================

Add:

Shared Notes

Members can contribute short notes during the Room.

Notes should show:
- author
- timestamp
- note text

Leader can optionally pin an important note.

Do not turn this into a document editor.

Keep it lightweight.

==================================================
5. SHARED ASK EMMAUS
==================================================

Add:

Ask Emmaus Together

When a leader or permitted participant asks a question in shared mode:

- Ask Emmaus receives Room context
- current content
- current Step
- current Scripture
- current discussion context

The answer appears to everyone in the Room.

Do NOT replace the existing private Ask Emmaus experience.

There are now two modes:

Private Ask Emmaus
Shared Ask Emmaus

Make the difference obvious.

Shared answers should use the existing Emmaus pastoral and theological response parameters.

At the bottom, continue to suggest relevant:
- Scriptures
- Daily Rhythm
- Walks
- Journeys
- Sermons
- Bible Studies

==================================================
6. POLLS
==================================================

Authorized Leader can launch a simple poll.

Examples:

Which statement best describes where you are today?

What stood out most?

Should we spend more time on this passage?

Poll types:

- Multiple choice
- Yes / No

Keep V1 simple.

Members vote once.

Show aggregate results to the Room after voting or when the leader reveals results.

Do not introduce scores or competition.

==================================================
7. PRAYER MODE
==================================================

Leader taps:

Prayer Time

Room UI shifts focus to Prayer Requests.

Video remains available but becomes visually secondary.

Show:

PRAYER TIME

Current Room Prayer Requests

Leader can move through requests one at a time.

Members can add requests during Prayer Mode.

Provide:

Return to Study

This restores the normal Room view.

==================================================
8. DISCUSSION MODE
==================================================

Leader taps:

Start Discussion

Surface:

- current discussion prompt from the content
- Group Discussion
- shared notes
- optional Ask Emmaus Together

Do not open a disconnected chat page unless the user explicitly chooses full discussion history.

The current study context must remain visible.

==================================================
9. ATTENDANCE
==================================================

Record Room session attendance automatically when a user meaningfully joins the live Room session.

Track:

- sessionId
- roomId
- userId
- joinedAt
- leftAt
- duration if appropriate

Do not infer spiritual engagement from attendance.

Admin/leader can later view:

Attended
Joined late
Left early
Did not attend

Keep this pastoral and factual.

==================================================
10. SESSION PLAN
==================================================

Use the existing Session Plan concept.

For example:

Welcome
Today's Step
Read Scripture
Discussion
Prayer
Next Step
Complete Session

Leader can move freely between stages.

Do not enforce prescribed times.

==================================================
11. COMPLETE SESSION
==================================================

Leader taps:

Complete Session

Show:

Session Complete

✓ Studied
✓ Discussed
✓ Prayed

Next Step Ready

Record:
- session completion
- attendance
- current group position
- Room activity
- prayer requests
- relevant shared notes

Do not delete anything.

==================================================
REAL-TIME ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Use a real-time event layer for:

- leader navigation
- scripture changes
- follow-leader state
- focus verse
- shared highlights
- shared notes
- shared Ask Emmaus answers
- polls
- prayer mode
- discussion mode
- session state

Use the existing architecture where possible.

If LiveKit data messages are appropriate for ephemeral real-time events, use them.

Persist important state in the database so reconnecting users can restore the current session.

Do not rely only on browser memory.

==================================================
RECONNECTION
==================================================

If a user temporarily loses connection:

- reconnect gracefully
- restore current Room session
- restore current Step
- restore current Scripture
- restore current mode
- restore shared notes/highlights
- rejoin video if appropriate

Do not lose Room state.

==================================================
MOBILE UX
==================================================

This must work cleanly on mobile.

Avoid side-by-side layouts that become unusable.

Use panels/sheets that open over the Room while keeping context.

Examples:

Open Scripture
Shared Notes
Prayer Time
Discussion
Poll

Everything should be reachable with large touch targets.

==================================================
DO NOT BUILD
==================================================

Still exclude:

- synchronized Bible audio
- synchronized sermon playback
- whiteboard
- Emmaus Voice participating as a live group participant
- complex media synchronization

These are deliberately deferred.

==================================================
TESTING
==================================================

Test with at least two simultaneous authenticated users.

Verify:

1. Leader starts Gathering.
2. Member joins.
3. Leader opens Scripture.
4. Member automatically receives it.
5. Member disables Follow Leader and can browse independently.
6. Member re-enables Follow Leader.
7. Leader opens today's Step.
8. Member follows.
9. Member highlights a verse.
10. Leader sees the highlight.
11. Leader sets Focus Verse.
12. Member sees it.
13. Shared note appears to both users.
14. Shared Ask Emmaus answer appears to both users.
15. Poll works for both users.
16. Prayer Mode moves both users into prayer-focused UI.
17. Return to Study restores normal Room.
18. Attendance records both users.
19. Leader completes session.
20. Session state remains stored after refresh.
21. Ordinary members cannot access leader-only controls.
22. LiveKit credentials remain server-side.
23. Reconnecting user restores current Room state.

==================================================
SUCCESS CONDITION
==================================================

The finished experience should allow a church leader to guide a small group through an Emmaus Walk, Journey, Devotional, Bible Study or Sermon Companion without saying:

"Can everyone turn to..."
"Can everyone find..."
"Can everyone see my screen..."

Emmaus should already keep the group together.

The technology should disappear into the background.

Build the full interactive Room experience.
