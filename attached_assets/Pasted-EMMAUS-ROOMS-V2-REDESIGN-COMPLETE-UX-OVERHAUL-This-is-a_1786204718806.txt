THE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Emmaus Rooms is NOT a chat application.

It is NOT a Zoom clone.

It is NOT a Discord server.

A Room exists for one reason:

To help people walk through today's Emmaus content together.

Every screen should reinforce that purpose.
