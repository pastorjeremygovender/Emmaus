5. USE HYBRID SERMON RETRIEVAL
==================================================

Sermon discovery must not depend on one exact keyword or embeddings alone.

Use hybrid retrieval combining:

- semantic/vector relevance
- full-text/keyword matching
- sermon title matching
- Scripture-reference matching
- transcript matching
- sermon-topic/tag matching where available

A query such as “Prodigal” must be able to retrieve sermons containing or substantially discussing:

- Prodigal Son
- Luke 15
- forgiveness
- restoration
- returning to God
- the Father’s welcome

Explicit Scripture references should strongly boost sermons linked to that passage.

Do not use broad synonym expansion to attach irrelevant sermons.

Apply a meaningful relevance threshold, but do not set it so high that genuine ICC content is routinely excluded.

For every substantive Ask Emmaus question:

2. Search eligible Local Church resources, including sermons.
