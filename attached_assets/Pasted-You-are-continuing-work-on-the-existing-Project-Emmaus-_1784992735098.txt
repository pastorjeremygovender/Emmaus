↓

2. Daily Devotional

↓

↓

DAILY GATE

Before today's 15 Minutes with Jesus has been completed:

Normal Journeys remain visible.

Do NOT hide them.

Do NOT disable them harshly.

Instead the primary action becomes:

Complete today's 15 Minutes with Jesus

Supporting text:

Begin with today's time with Jesus.

Your Journey will be ready afterwards.

Selecting this simply opens today's 15 Minutes with Jesus.

Never display an error.

Never use guilt language.

=====================================================

AFTER COMPLETION

No refresh.

No reload.

No logout/login.
