BIBLE READER UX CLEANUP — MOBILE PILOT

The current Bible reader has four UX problems:

1. The chapter overview card at the top duplicates content already available inside the verse Study panel.
2. The main bottom navigation is missing while reading a chapter.
3. The translation copyright notice takes up too much space on the My Bible home screen.
4. The floating Ask Emmaus button blocks the final lines of Scripture.

Do not redesign the Bible.
Do not change Bible text, translation switching, chapter navigation, Study, Preached Here, Notes, Save, or verse selection.

==================================================
1. REMOVE THE CHAPTER OVERVIEW CARD
==================================================

Remove the large chapter overview card from the top of the chapter reader.

Do not delete the overview content from the database.

The overview should remain available inside the existing Study section when a member taps a verse.

Replace the removed card with one compact permanent helper line beneath the chapter title:

“Tap any verse to open Bible Study options.”

Requirements:

- one line where possible;
- small, calm secondary text;
- no card;
- no border;
- no large vertical spacing;
- remain visible at the top of every Bible chapter;
- do not repeat while scrolling.

==================================================
2. RESTORE MAIN APP NAVIGATION
==================================================

The standard bottom navigation must remain available while reading the Bible chapter:

- Today’s Steps
- Next Steps
- My Bible
- My Journey

My Bible must remain highlighted.

The chapter navigation:

- previous chapter
- current chapter
- next chapter

must remain directly above the main app navigation.

Do not replace one navigation system with the other.

Required bottom order:

Scripture content
→ chapter navigation
→ standard app navigation

Add enough bottom padding so Scripture and chapter controls are never hidden behind either navigation bar.

==================================================
3. COLLAPSE TRANSLATION INFORMATION
==================================================

On the My Bible home screen, replace the full expanded copyright/licensing block with a compact summary.

Default collapsed state should show approximately two lines:

“Good News Translation (GNT)  
© 1992 American Bible Society”

Add a small downward chevron or “Translation details” control.

When tapped, expand to show the full required licensing text.

When tapped again, collapse it.

Requirements:

- collapsed by default;
- full legal text remains accessible;
- no legal wording may be deleted;
- remember the collapsed state during normal navigation;
- do not allow the expanded notice to push essential Bible controls off-screen unnecessarily.

Apply the same reusable disclosure pattern to other translations where licensing text exists.

==================================================
4. FIX ASK EMMAUS OVERLAP
==================================================

The floating Ask Emmaus button currently blocks the last line of Scripture and can overlap controls.

Fix this across the entire Bible experience.

Preferred behaviour:

- keep the existing button design;
- place it above the chapter navigation and main app navigation;
- reserve sufficient content padding beneath Scripture;
- never cover a verse, the last line, previous/next chapter controls, translation controls, or bottom navigation.

Do not merely reduce opacity.

Do not hide Scripture behind the button.

If required, make the button gently move upward when approaching fixed bottom controls, but do not create distracting animation.

==================================================
5. RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOUR
==================================================

Verify on:

- normal Android phone;
- smaller phone;
- tablet;
- portrait orientation;
- landscape orientation where supported.

Only responsive spacing may differ.

The component structure and functionality must remain the same.
