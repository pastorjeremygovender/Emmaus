==================================================
IMPLEMENTATION
==================================================

1. LATEST MESSAGE

The MockProvider must identify the most recent user message correctly.

It must not:

- use the first user message as the current question
- use the most recent assistant response as the user question
- select a response only by conversation length
- cycle through unrelated canned responses

2. INTENT-BASED DEVELOPMENT RESPONSES

Create a small, explicit development intent map for testing Ask Emmaus.

At minimum, support:

A. NAME / IDENTITY

Questions such as:

- Why are you called Emmaus?
- Where does the name Emmaus come from?
- What does Emmaus mean?

Return a relevant response explaining:

- the name comes from Luke 24
- two disciples were walking on the road to Emmaus
- Jesus came alongside them
- He opened the Scriptures and helped them recognise Him
- the platform name reflects helping people walk with Jesus
- Emmaus is technology, not a human or spiritual being

Use the official mission wording exactly:

“Emmaus exists because we believe technology can serve a faithful purpose when it is designed and used wisely to help people walk with Jesus Christ.”

Do not claim that Emmaus believes, loves, prays, feels, hears God, or possesses spiritual authority.

B. FEELING FAR FROM GOD

Questions such as:

- I feel far from God
- God feels distant
- I do not feel close to God

Return the existing appropriate Psalm 42 development response.

C. FORGIVENESS

Questions explicitly about:

- forgiveness
- bitterness
- forgiving someone

Return the forgiveness development response.

D. GENERAL GREETING

Questions such as:

- Hi
- Hello

Return a brief, calm greeting that invites the person to share what they are working through without saying:

- Anything else?
- What else can I help with?

E. UNKNOWN QUESTION

For an unsupported development question, return an honest fallback:

“Ask Emmaus is currently using a limited development response provider. Your question was received correctly, but this test provider does not yet have a suitable response for it.”

Do not return an unrelated theological answer.

3. MATCHING RULES

Use clear, deterministic matching.

- Normalise case.
- Trim punctuation and whitespace where appropriate.
- Prefer specific intent matches before general matches.
- Do not match “forgiveness” unless forgiveness-related language is actually present.
- Do not use loose matching that causes unrelated questions to trigger the wrong topic.
- Keep the intent map separated from UI code.

4. CONVERSATION CONTEXT

Verify that the full conversation history reaches the provider.

The latest user message must determine the immediate response.

Earlier conversation content may provide context, but must not override a clear new question.

Examples:

Conversation:
I feel far from God.
Why are you called Emmaus?

The second response must answer why the platform is called Emmaus.

Conversation:
I feel far from God.
Can you explain Psalm 42 again?

The second response may use the earlier Psalm 42 context.
