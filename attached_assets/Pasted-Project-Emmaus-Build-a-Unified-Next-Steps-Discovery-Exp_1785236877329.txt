Project Emmaus — Build a Unified Next Steps Discovery Experience

The member experience currently has a discovery gap.

Current problems:

1. Published Daily Devotional series cannot be browsed or selected by members.
2. Published Sermon Devotionals cannot be browsed or selected by members.
3. A newly generated and published Sermon Devotional cannot be found anywhere unless it is already surfaced automatically.
4. The Next Steps tab is still too Journey-focused.
5. On Today’s Steps, the labels currently say:
   - Your Journeys
   - Explore Journeys

These must become:

- Your Next Steps
- Explore Next Steps

The Next Steps area must become the unified member discovery experience for:

- Daily Devotionals
- Sermon Devotionals
- Journeys
- Bible Studies
- other future intentional discipleship content

Do not merge the underlying content types.
Do not merge their progress systems.
Do not treat Daily Devotionals or Sermon Devotionals as Journeys.
Do not redesign Daily Rhythm.
Do not auto-start content for members.

====================================================
LOCKED MEMBER NAVIGATION
====================================================

Bottom navigation remains:

Today’s Steps

Next Steps

My Bible

My Journey

Next Steps answers:

“What could I intentionally begin next?”

Today’s Steps answers:

“What am I already walking through with Jesus today?”

====================================================
TASK 1 — UPDATE TODAY’S STEPS TERMINOLOGY
====================================================

On Today’s Steps, replace:

Your Journeys

with:

Your Next Steps

Replace:

Explore Journeys →

with:

Explore Next Steps →

The Explore Next Steps action must open the main Next Steps tab.

Do not link it directly to Journeys only.

====================================================
TASK 2 — TODAY’S STEPS CONTENT RULES
====================================================

Today’s Steps must display only active or current content.

Approved order:

1. 10 Minutes with Jesus

2. Active Daily Devotional

3. Your Next Steps

4. This Week’s Sermon Devotional

5. Floating Ask Emmaus

“Your Next Steps” may contain active content the member has intentionally started, including:

- Journeys
- Bible Studies
- other structured pathways

Do not automatically include every published item.

If the member has not begun any structured Next Step, show only:

Explore Next Steps →

Daily Devotional remains its own card beneath 10 Minutes with Jesus when the member has started a series.

The current church Sermon Devotional remains its own card lower on Today’s Steps when it has been marked as current and published.

====================================================
TASK 3 — REDESIGN THE NEXT STEPS TAB
====================================================

The Next Steps tab must no longer be a Journey-only page.

Create a clean discovery page with the heading:

Next Steps

Supporting text:

Choose something that will help you take your next step with Jesus.

Use clearly separated sections.

Recommended section order:

1. Recommended for You

2. Daily Devotionals

3. This Week’s Sermon

4. Journeys

5. Bible Studies

6. Recently Added

Only show a section when it has eligible published content.

Do not show empty sections.

====================================================
TASK 4 — RECOMMENDED FOR YOU
====================================================

This section may display a small number of relevant published items using existing recommendation logic.

Do not introduce a complex recommendation algorithm.

Initially use simple rules such as:

- current church Sermon Devotional;
- a beginner Journey if the member has not started one;
- an available Daily Devotional series if none is active;
- recently published content.

Do not use popularity metrics, public rankings or social engagement.

====================================================
TASK 5 — DAILY DEVOTIONAL DISCOVERY
====================================================

Create a Daily Devotionals section inside Next Steps.

It must list all member-visible published Daily Devotional series.

Example:

Psalms Daily Devotional

Walk through the Psalms one day at a time, discovering how every season of life can draw you closer to God.

14 Days or Ongoing

Begin Devotional

When more series are added, they must appear automatically.

Possible future examples:

- Proverbs Daily Devotional
- Seasonal Devotional
- General Daily Devotional
- Church-Specific Devotional

Do not hard-code only Psalms.

Eligibility rules:

- series status is Published;
- at least one entry is Published;
- publication window is valid where used;
- series is marked available to members.

====================================================
TASK 6 — DAILY DEVOTIONAL START FLOW
====================================================

When a member opens a Daily Devotional series, show:

- series title;
- description;
- available length or “Ongoing”;
- first entry title where appropriate;
- primary action.

If not started:

Begin Devotional

If already active:

Continue Devotional

If completed for today:

Review Today

Starting must:

- create or reuse a separate Daily Devotional progress record;
- set the member’s local start date;
- make Day 1 available;
- add the Daily Devotional card to Today’s Steps;
- never create Journey progress.

A member should normally have one active Daily Devotional series displayed on Today’s Steps.

If the architecture permits multiple started series, provide a clear method for choosing which one is current without losing progress.

Do not silently replace an active series.

====================================================
TASK 7 — SERMON DEVOTIONAL DISCOVERY
====================================================

Create a section in Next Steps titled:

This Week’s Sermon

When there is a current published Sermon Devotional, show it prominently.

Card content:

This Week’s Sermon

Sermon title

Speaker

Main Scripture

Companion length

Short summary

Primary action:

Begin Sermon Devotional

or:

Continue Sermon Devotional

Secondary action:

View Sermon

The new “Jesus at the Center” Sermon Devotional must become visible here when:

- the Sermon is published;
- the Companion is published;
- at least one Companion entry is published;
- it is marked as current or member-visible;
- its publication window is valid.

Do not generate member visibility from Sermon Draft status.

====================================================
TASK 8 — PREVIOUS SERMON DEVOTIONALS
====================================================

Below the current Sermon section, provide a secondary action such as:

Browse Previous Sermons →

This opens a list of published member-visible Sermon Devotionals.

Each item may show:

- sermon title;
- speaker;
- date;
- main Scripture;
- companion length;
- status for the member:
  - Not Started
  - In Progress
  - Completed

Members may intentionally begin an older Sermon Devotional.

Do not automatically place every old Sermon Devotional on Today’s Steps.

Only the member’s active companion or the officially current companion should surface there according to the established rules.

====================================================
TASK 9 — SERMON DEVOTIONAL START FLOW
====================================================

When a member begins a Sermon Devotional:

- create a separate Sermon Companion progress record;
- set Day 1 as the current available entry;
- keep progress separate from Journeys;
- add the active Sermon Devotional to Today’s Steps where appropriate;
- preserve calendar or publication rules;
- do not auto-publish;
- do not create Journey progress.

If the church marks a new sermon as current while a member is still completing an older one, do not silently erase the older progress.

Use a safe rule such as:

- Today’s Steps prioritises the current church Sermon Devotional;
- previously started companions remain accessible from Next Steps or My Journey;
- progress is preserved.

Keep the member experience uncluttered.

====================================================

Create or correct one canonical Next Steps discovery API or query layer.

Suggested response shape:

====================================================
TASK 15 — PUBLICATION AND VISIBILITY RULES
====================================================

A content item must never appear in Next Steps merely because an admin record exists.

Daily Devotional requires:

- Published series;
- Published entry;
- valid availability.

Sermon Devotional requires:

- Published Companion;
- Published entry;
- member-visible/current setting;
- valid publication window.

Journey requires:

- Published Journey;
- at least one valid Published day.

Bible Study requires:

- Published Journey with Journey Type Bible Study;
- at least one valid Published day.

Do not return Draft or Archived content to normal members.

Development Mode may view all published test content according to existing bypass rules.

This Week’s Sermon Devotional

- clear the previous current assignment;
- preserve the previous content and member progress;
- update Next Steps;
- update Today’s Steps;

Do not make publication alone automatically mark it as current unless that is explicitly selected.
