SPRINT NAME

Daily Rhythm Architecture

=====================================================

VISION

15 Minutes with Jesus is no longer a normal Journey.

It becomes the permanent Daily Rhythm of Emmaus.

Every member begins every day here.

It is the heartbeat of the platform.

Journeys begin and end.

Daily Rhythm continues for life.

=====================================================

CORE PRINCIPLE

Every day when a member opens Emmaus they should immediately know their next step.

The Daily Rhythm always appears first.

Emmaus should never ask:

"What would you like to do today?"

Instead it gently says:

"This is today's next step."
