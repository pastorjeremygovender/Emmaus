Fix the Emmaus meeting controls and mobile device-check UI. This is a functional correction, not merely a visual polish.

CURRENT PROBLEMS CONFIRMED BY SCREENSHOTS

1. The “Check your devices” panel does not fit on mobile:
   - The Join gathering button extends beyond the right edge.
   - Content is too wide.
   - Ask Emmaus overlaps the panel.
   - Controls do not adapt to narrow screens.

2. An audio-only meeting is unnecessarily checking and previewing the camera.

3. Once inside the audio meeting, the user cannot mute the microphone.

4. There are empty white control boxes in the live-audio panel. These appear to be microphone and device controls, but their content is invisible and/or disabled.

5. Microphone, camera and hand-raise controls are not available on all meeting screens. When the participant opens the Bible, all meeting controls disappear.

REQUIRED FIX

A. MAKE DEVICE CHECK MODE-AWARE

Determine the meeting type before rendering or requesting permissions.

For an AUDIO meeting:
- Show only:
  - Mic on / Mic off
  - Listen only
  - Microphone selector when available
  - Microphone level test
  - Speaker/output check where supported
- Do not request camera permission.
- Do not enumerate camera devices.
- Do not display a camera selector.
- Do not start a camera preview.
- Do not require camera permission before joining.
- “Check devices” must check audio devices only.
- A user choosing “Listen only” must be able to join without granting microphone or camera permission.

For a VIDEO meeting:
- Show microphone and camera controls.
- Check microphone and camera separately.
- Allow the user to join with the camera off.
- Camera permission must not be required when joining camera-off.
- Clearly report each permission independently.

For a TEXT meeting:
- Do not request microphone or camera permissions.

Never call getUserMedia with video enabled for an audio-only meeting.

B. RESTORE WORKING MICROPHONE CONTROL

The microphone button must work before and after joining.

Required states:
- Mic on
- Mic muted
- Permission needed
- Microphone unavailable
- Listen only

When tapped:
- Mic on → immediately mute the published local audio track.
- Mic muted → immediately unmute or republish the existing local audio track.
- Do not recreate the entire meeting connection for every mute/unmute action.
- Keep the visible state synchronized with the actual LiveKit/local participant track state.
- Do not permanently disable the button merely because device check has completed.
- If permission was denied, display a clear “Allow microphone in device settings” message instead of an empty or disabled button.

Verify whether the control has accidentally inherited:
- `disabled`
- `pointer-events: none`
- low opacity
- white text/icon on a white background
- an invisible overlay blocking taps
- an incorrect condition tied to camera permission
- an incorrect condition tied to meeting type

C. FIX THE EMPTY WHITE BOXES

Identify the purpose of every control currently rendered as an empty white box.

Do not leave icon-only white controls with invisible white icons.

Use clear labelled controls:
- “Mute” / “Unmute”
- “Devices”
- “Camera off” / “Camera on” in video meetings only
- “Lower hand” / “Raise hand”

Every control must have:
- a visible icon
- a visible text label where space permits
- sufficient contrast
- a minimum 44 × 44px touch target
- a clear active/inactive state
- an accessible label
- a tooltip or title on larger screens

Do not hide a control’s meaning through colour alone.

D. ADD UNIVERSAL MEETING CONTROLS

While a participant is connected to an active meeting, show a persistent compact meeting-control dock on EVERY screen they can access during the meeting, including:

- meeting overview
- chat
- Group Notes
- Today’s Study
- presented content
- shared images or slides
- My Bible
- Bible chapter reader
- Bible Study view
- Ask Emmaus
- any full-screen or expanded meeting view

The persistent dock must contain:

For AUDIO meetings:
- Mute / Unmute microphone
- Raise / Lower hand
- Return to meeting
- Leave meeting

For VIDEO meetings:
- Mute / Unmute microphone
- Camera on / Camera off
- Raise / Lower hand
- Return to meeting
- Leave meeting

For LISTEN-ONLY participants:
- Join microphone / remain listen-only
- Raise / Lower hand
- Return to meeting
- Leave meeting

The controls must operate on the same shared meeting state and LiveKit room instance. Do not create a second connection when navigating between screens.

The dock must remain visible when the Bible is opened. Screenshot 4 confirms that it is currently completely absent from the Bible screen.

Use a global meeting-controls component mounted at the authenticated app layout or meeting-aware root level—not inside only the meeting card or one meeting route.

It must survive route changes and must not unmount/reconnect the participant when navigating.

E. MOBILE LAYOUT

Correct the device-check layout for narrow screens.

At widths below 480px:
- All content must remain within the viewport.
- Use `width: 100%` with appropriate inner padding.
- Use `max-width: 100%`.
- Use `box-sizing: border-box`.
- Prevent horizontal page overflow.
- Stack action buttons vertically if they cannot fit comfortably in one row.
- The Join gathering button must be fully visible and tappable.
- Mic on and Listen only may wrap or become a two-column grid only if both fit.
- Device selectors must use the available width.
- Preview areas must scale responsively.
- Long labels must wrap safely.
- The panel must not sit underneath the fixed bottom navigation.

Do not solve the overflow by shrinking text or touch targets excessively.

F. PREVENT FLOATING-UI COLLISIONS

Ask Emmaus is overlapping the device check and meeting content.

While a device-check panel, modal, or persistent meeting dock is present:
- reposition or temporarily collapse the Ask Emmaus floating button
- ensure it does not cover Join, Cancel, Check devices, microphone controls, hand raise or Leave
- account for both the app bottom navigation and Android safe-area inset

Use coordinated bottom offsets rather than unrelated hard-coded positions.

G. PRESERVE MEETING TYPE

Do not silently turn an audio meeting into a video-capable device check.

Carry the meeting mode consistently through:
- scheduled meeting record
- join screen
- permission request
- device check
- LiveKit track publication
- in-meeting controls
- persistent control dock

Acceptance tests:

1. AUDIO MEETING
- Open an audio meeting on a narrow Android screen.
- No camera permission is requested.
- No camera selector or preview is shown.
- Check devices checks microphone/audio only.
- Join button fits fully within the screen.
- Join with Mic on.
- Tap Mute: the participant is actually muted and the label changes to Unmute.
- Tap Unmute: audio resumes and the label changes to Mute.
- Navigate to My Bible: the compact meeting controls remain visible.
- Mute/unmute and raise/lower hand from the Bible screen.
- Return to the meeting without reconnecting.
- Leave successfully.

2. LISTEN ONLY
- Choose Listen only.
- Join without granting microphone or camera permission.
- No fake microphone-on state is shown.
- Allow the participant to activate the microphone later, requesting permission only then.

3. VIDEO MEETING
- Microphone and camera controls are shown.
- Join with camera off.
- Toggle camera and microphone independently.
- Both controls remain accessible while viewing the Bible or other shared content.

4. TEXT MEETING
- No audio or camera permission prompt appears.

5. MOBILE UI
Test at 320px, 360px, 390px and 412px widths:
- no horizontal overflow
- no clipped Join button
- no empty controls
- no overlap from Ask Emmaus
- no overlap with bottom navigation
- all controls have visible labels or clearly visible icons

6. STATE
- Navigating between meeting, Bible and content screens does not disconnect or create duplicate LiveKit participants.
- Microphone, camera and hand state remains accurate across every screen.

Before finishing:
- Search for all meeting-control implementations and remove conflicting duplicate local state.
- Identify exactly what created the two empty white boxes.
- Report the root cause of the broken mute button.
- List the files changed.
- Report the result of every acceptance test.
