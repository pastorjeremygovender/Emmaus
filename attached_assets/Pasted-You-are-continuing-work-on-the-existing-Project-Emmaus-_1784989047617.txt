=====================================================
SPRINT NAME

Member Journeys Experience

=====================================================
SPRINT GOAL

Redesign the member-facing Journeys page so it feels calm, pastoral and easy to understand.

The page should answer one question:

“What Journeys am I walking through, and what could help me grow next?”

The page must not feel like a generic content library, admin list or dashboard.

It should help users:

- Continue active Journeys
- Discover a small number of relevant Journeys
- Explore the wider Journey catalogue when they choose
- Understand the commitment and rhythm of each Journey
- Start, pause and resume Journeys clearly
- Stay within the existing two-active-Journey limit

=====================================================
CORE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

The Walk screen guides the user through today.

The Journeys screen helps the user manage and discover longer discipleship pathways.

Do not duplicate content already featured on the Walk screen.

Do not place This Week’s Sermon Devotional on the Journeys page.

Do not place Daily Devotional on the Journeys page.

Do not place 15 Minutes with Jesus on the Journeys page as a normal Journey card.

Do not place Rooms on the Journeys page.

=====================================================
REMOVE FROM CURRENT PAGE

Remove the following from the member-facing Journeys page:

- This Week section
- This Week’s Sermon or Sermon Companion card
- My Rooms section
- Walk with others card
- Any duplicate Ask Emmaus card
- Any duplicated progress summary already shown on Walk
- Any generic “No active Journeys” block that leaves the screen feeling empty

Keep the floating Ask Emmaus button if it already appears globally.

Do not remove Rooms functionality from the application.

Simply remove Rooms content from this page.

Use:

Journeys

Supporting line:

Grow through guided discipleship.

Keep the introduction short.

Do not use:

Continue active walks, revisit completed ones, and explore what’s next.

The new wording should feel simpler and warmer.

=====================================================
NO ACTIVE JOURNEYS STATE

Button:

Do not add guilt or pressure.

=====================================================
ACTIVE JOURNEY LIMIT

Preserve the maximum of two active non-exempt Journeys per user.

Show the active capacity gently where useful.

Example:

1 of 2 active Journeys

or

2 active Journeys

Avoid making it feel like a storage quota.

Do not show the limit prominently when the user has no reason to think about it.

When the user tries to begin a third active Journey, preserve the warm dialog:

You already have two Journeys underway.

To help you focus and finish well, pause one before beginning another.

Buttons:

Review my Journeys

Not now

Review my Journeys should keep the user within the Journeys experience and allow them to pause one active Journey.

Pausing must preserve all progress.

=====================================================
PAUSED JOURNEYS

Paused Journeys should not appear in Continue Your Journeys.

They should be accessible through a secondary filter or tab in the wider Journeys area.

Use a simple view structure such as:

- Active
- Paused
- Completed
- Saved

Do not make these the first thing users see unless they actively open the management view.

Paused Journey cards should show:

- Journey title
- Last Step reached
- Paused label
- Resume button
- View details

Resuming must return the user to the exact correct Step.

Do not recommend a Journey already active or paused as though it is new.
