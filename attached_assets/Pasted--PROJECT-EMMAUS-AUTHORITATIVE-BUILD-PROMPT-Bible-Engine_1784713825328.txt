# PROJECT EMMAUS – AUTHORITATIVE BUILD PROMPT
## Bible Engine v1.1 – Final Pre-Merge Revision

The Bible Engine is now functionally complete. Before applying these changes to the main branch, perform one final UX and architecture refinement.

These instructions supersede previous UX decisions while preserving all completed engineering work.

---

PRIMARY DESIGN PRINCIPLE

"The Bible is not one of Emmaus' features.

It is one of Emmaus' destinations.

Every feature should lead people into Scripture.

Once people are reading Scripture, the interface should quietly step aside."

The Bible should feel peaceful, uncluttered and centred entirely on God's Word.

---

1. SEPARATE WALK FROM BIBLE

Refactor the navigation philosophy.

WALK

Walk is the discipleship dashboard.

It answers:

"What is God inviting me to do today?"

Walk should contain:

• Active Journeys
• Today's Journey Scripture
• Continue Journey
• Prayer prompts
• Reflection
• Journey progress
• Daily discipleship actions

Users may have multiple active journeys simultaneously.

Example:

15 Minutes with Jesus
Today's Scripture
Luke 6
Continue →

Serving Journey
Acts 6
Continue →

Marriage Journey
Ephesians 5
Continue →

Walk is responsible for multiple journeys.

The Bible is not.

---

BIBLE

Bible answers only one question:

"What am I reading in God's Word?"

The Bible should contain ONLY:

• Continue Reading
• Browse Books
• Search (future-ready placeholder)
• Bookmarks
• Reading Plans (future-ready placeholder)
• Translation selector
• Reading history

Do NOT place Journey cards inside the Bible.

Do NOT display discipleship reminders inside the Bible.

Do NOT clutter the Bible with dashboard functionality.

---

2. REMOVE DUPLICATE READING CARDS

Never display both:

Continue Reading

and

Today's Reading

when they refer to the same chapter.

Rules:

First-time reader:

Display only:

Walk Through Luke

Luke 1

Read

---

Returning reader:

Display only:

Continue Reading

Current Chapter

Continue

There should always be ONE obvious next step.

---

3. LUKE REMAINS THE FLAGSHIP GOSPEL

Luke permanently replaces John as the onboarding Gospel.

John remains available as a normal Bible book.

Remove any remaining John-first assumptions.

---

4. PRESERVE COMPLETED ENGINEERING

Do NOT break:

• Bible Provider abstraction
• Translation architecture
• Bookmarks
• Reading progress
• Browse Books
• Continue Reading
• Reading history
• 66-book architecture

Only simplify the user experience.

---

5. PREPARE FOR FUTURE BIBLE FEATURES

Do NOT place these on the Bible Home.

Instead prepare clean extension points inside the chapter reading experience for:

• Ask Emmaus
• Go Deeper
• Preached Here
• Live It

These belong within the reading experience, not on the Bible Home.

---

6. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

When making UX decisions always choose:

Less clutter.

Less duplication.

Less noise.

Less navigation.

More Scripture.

More peace.

More clarity.

More focus.

Technology should disappear.

Christ should become more visible.

---

SUCCESS CRITERIA

Walk becomes the home of discipleship.

Bible becomes the home of Scripture.

Journeys becomes the home of intentional growth.

Personal becomes the home of the individual.

A first-time user should immediately know what to do.

A mature believer should feel that Scripture—not the interface—is the centre of the experience.

Perform a final regression review, ensure all existing functionality remains intact, then apply the changes to the main branch.
