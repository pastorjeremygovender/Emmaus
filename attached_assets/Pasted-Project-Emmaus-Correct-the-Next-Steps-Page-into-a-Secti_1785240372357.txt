Project Emmaus — Correct the Next Steps Page into a Sectioned Discovery Experience

The current Next Steps page is incorrect.

Current problems:

- The page opens with “In Progress” as the main emphasis.
- Psalms Daily Devotional appears again without a clear content category.
- Published Bible devotional/Bible Study content is not visible.
- The page still feels like a progress screen instead of a discovery screen.
- The sectioned layout previously approved has not been implemented correctly.

Fix the Next Steps page completely.

Do not redesign Today’s Steps.
Do not merge content types.
Do not treat Daily Devotionals, Sermon Devotionals or Bible Studies as Journeys.
Do not show duplicate cards.

====================================================
LOCKED PURPOSE
====================================================

Next Steps answers:

“What could I intentionally begin next?”

It is a discovery page.

It is not primarily:

- an In Progress page;
- a completion-history page;
- a Journey-only page;
- a duplicate of Today’s Steps.

Member progress belongs under My Journey or within the individual content cards.

Next Steps

Remove “In Progress” as the main page heading.

1. Recommended for You

====================================================
CONTENT ELIGIBILITY
====================================================

Only return member-visible content.

Daily Devotional:

- series Published;
- at least one entry Published;
- member visibility enabled;
- valid publication window.

Sermon Devotional:

- Companion Published;
- at least one entry Published;
- member visibility enabled;
- current flag for This Week’s Sermon section.

Journey:

- Journey Published;
- at least one day Published;
- Journey Type is not Bible Study.

Bible Study:

- Journey Published;
- at least one day Published;
- Journey Type is Bible Study.

Draft and Archived records must not appear for normal members.

Development Mode may follow existing testing permissions.

====================================================
FIX THE MISSING BIBLE CONTENT
====================================================

Trace the new Bible devotional/Bible Study record.

Inspect:

- its database ID;
- status;
- Journey Type or content type;
- member-visible setting;
- published days;
- collection;
- publication dates;
- discovery API filtering.

Identify why it is excluded.

Possible causes:

- record still Draft;
- day entries still Draft;
- missing member-visible flag;
- wrong Journey Type;
- discovery query only loads standard Journeys;
- content incorrectly classified as Daily Devotional;
- API filters by an obsolete content type;
- missing Collection relationship.

Correct the real cause.

Do not hard-code this one record into the frontend.

All future published Bible Studies must appear automatically.
