=====================================================
PARAGRAPH SPACING

The current content renderer creates too much vertical space because many individual lines are treated like separate paragraphs.

Fix this globally in the shared Daily Rhythm reader.

Requirements:

- Sentences within the same thought should read as a normal paragraph.
- Do not insert large margins between every line.
- Preserve intentional paragraph breaks authored in Content Studio.
- Use normal body line-height.
- Use modest paragraph spacing.
- Avoid a presentation-slide appearance.

Example:

Good morning, Jeremy.

I’m glad you’re back.

Yesterday, Jesus invited you to come and see. Today, you’ll discover something even more personal. He already knows you.

Do not display every sentence as a separate widely spaced block unless the editor intentionally inserted a paragraph break.

Apply this to:

- Greeting
- Reflection
- Prayer
- Your Next Step
- Closing
- Member view
- Content Studio preview
- Replay mode
