# EMMAUS ROOMS — FOUR-LEVEL ARCHITECTURE + BUILD

We are now establishing the permanent Emmaus Rooms architecture.

Do NOT rebuild existing Rooms from scratch.

Audit the existing Rooms implementation first and preserve working functionality, existing Rooms, members, invitations, discussions, progress and data.

The purpose of this update is to establish FOUR clear levels of Rooms and simplify how users encounter them.

====================================================
CORE PRINCIPLE
====================================================

Emmaus Rooms exist to help people walk with Jesus together.

Rooms are NOT a social network.

Rooms are NOT a general-purpose video calling service.

Video is a ministry capability and must be controlled by church permissions.

Ordinary users should still be able to walk through Emmaus content together without requiring video.

====================================================
THE FOUR ROOM LEVELS
====================================================

LEVEL 1 — PERSONAL ROOMS
Available to normal Emmaus users.

Purpose:

Allow friends, families and individuals to walk through Emmaus content together.

NO LIVE VIDEO.

A Personal Room may include:

• Walks
• Journeys
• Daily Devotionals
• Bible Studies
• Sermon Companions
• Scripture
• Discussion
• Shared progress
• Prayer requests
• Text chat
• Shared notes where appropriate

A user should be able to create a Personal Room from content they are ALREADY doing.

Example:

Jeremy is on Day 6 of a devotional.

He decides it could help a friend.

He taps:

"Walk Together"

or another natural context-specific label.

Emmaus creates a Personal Room attached to that content.

Jeremy invites the friend.

Do NOT force Jeremy to abandon or restart his existing progress.

Handle individual and Room progress separately where necessary so existing personal progress is never lost.

Personal Rooms must NOT have LiveKit video capability.

====================================================
LEVEL 2 — MINISTRY ROOMS
====================================================

Ministry Rooms are church-authorised Rooms.

Examples:

• Small Groups
• Made Free
• New Believers
• Youth
• Men's Ministry
• Women's Ministry
• Prayer Groups
• Home Groups
• Discipleship Groups

These Rooms support everything in Personal Rooms PLUS:

• Live video
• Shared Scripture
• Shared highlighting
• Shared notes
• Leader controls
• Room attendance
• Polls
• Shared Ask Emmaus responses
• Real-time collaborative study

VIDEO PROVIDER:

Use LiveKit as the video infrastructure.

Do NOT build our own WebRTC/video engine.

Emmaus owns the Room experience.

LiveKit only provides the underlying real-time audio/video transport.

IMPORTANT:

Only an authorised Group Leader, Pastor or authorised Church Admin can START a LiveKit video session.

Normal members can JOIN an authorised meeting but cannot create/start video meetings unless granted the appropriate role.

====================================================
LEVEL 3 — LEADERSHIP ROOMS
====================================================

Leadership Rooms are restricted to authorised church leadership.

Examples:

• Pastors
• Elders
• Ministry Leaders
• Leadership Teams
• Staff

They may include:

• Video
• Scripture
• Notes
• Prayer
• Discussion
• Planning
• Private leadership communication
• Appropriate Emmaus collaboration tools

Leadership Rooms must have stricter membership permissions.

Do NOT expose Leadership Rooms through general Discover browsing.

Only invited/authorised users should see them.

====================================================
LEVEL 4 — CHURCH SERVICE ROOMS
====================================================

Create the architecture/Room type for Church Service Rooms but DO NOT build the full online congregation system yet.

This is reserved for the future Emmaus live church experience.

Future purpose:

Integrate the physical congregation and online congregation into one gathering rather than simply livestreaming a broadcast.

Do NOT build Media Studio integration now.

Do NOT build synchronized worship/audio now.

Do NOT build advanced production tools now.

For now:

Create the Room type and permission model only.

Hide unfinished Church Service Room functionality from normal users.

====================================================
ROLES AND PERMISSIONS
====================================================

Implement Room permissions based on church roles.

MEMBER

Can:

• Create Personal Rooms
• Invite people to Personal Rooms
• Join Rooms they are invited to
• Participate in discussion
• Share prayer requests
• Use shared content
• Join authorised video meetings

Cannot:

• Start LiveKit video
• Create Ministry Rooms
• Create Leadership Rooms
• Create Church Service Rooms

----------------------------------------------------

GROUP LEADER

Can:

Everything a Member can do PLUS:

• Create/manage authorised Ministry Rooms
• Start Ministry Room video
• Invite/remove members
• Use leader controls
• View Room attendance
• Moderate Room activity

Group Leader status must be assigned/approved by the church.

----------------------------------------------------

PASTOR

Can:

• Create/manage Ministry Rooms
• Create/manage Leadership Rooms
• Start authorised video
• Assign/manage appropriate Room leaders
• Use leader controls
• View Room attendance
• Moderate Rooms

----------------------------------------------------

CHURCH ADMIN

Can:

• Configure Room permissions
• Approve/remove Group Leader status
• Enable/disable video capability
• Configure video limits
• View video usage
• Manage Room policy

Do not assume every Admin automatically needs access to confidential pastoral content unless their permissions allow it.

====================================================
LIVEKIT COST CONTROL
====================================================

LiveKit usage must be controlled.

Add Church Settings for:

VIDEO ROOMS

Enabled: ON/OFF

Maximum concurrent video Rooms

Maximum participants per Room

Maximum meeting duration

Approved roles allowed to start video

Do not allow unlimited uncontrolled video usage.

The backend must verify permission BEFORE issuing a LiveKit access token capable of starting/hosting a meeting.

Do not rely only on hiding the Start Video button in the UI.

====================================================
ROOMS IN MY JOURNEY
====================================================

My Journey should NOT contain:

Create Room

Join Room

Those actions duplicate Discover/content actions.

Instead show:

MY ROOMS

If user belongs to no Rooms:

"Any Rooms you are part of will show up here."

Nothing else is required.

If the user belongs to Rooms:

Display those Rooms.

Show useful information such as:

Room name
Associated content
Leader
Current activity/progress
Next meeting if applicable

Tapping opens the Room.

====================================================
CREATING ROOMS FROM CONTENT
====================================================

Users should NOT have to think:

"I need to create a Room."

Rooms should emerge naturally from discipleship.

Add an appropriate action to:

• Walks
• Journeys
• Daily Devotionals
• Bible Studies
• Sermon Companions

Examples of contextual wording:

Walk Together
Study Together
Invite Someone

Do NOT clutter every screen with multiple Room buttons.

Use one consistent, unobtrusive action.

When tapped:

1. Ask who they want to invite.
2. Create the Personal Room.
3. Attach the current content.
4. Preserve the creator's existing progress.
5. Send invitation.
6. Room appears under My Journey → My Rooms.

If the user is an authorised Group Leader, allow an additional option:

"Create Ministry Room"

Do not show this option to ordinary members.

====================================================
MINISTRY ROOM EXPERIENCE
====================================================

The eventual Ministry Room screen should combine:

LIVE VIDEO

+

CURRENT EMMAUS CONTENT

The user should not feel like they have left Emmaus and entered Zoom.

The Room may contain:

Video participants

Current Walk/Devotional/Study

Scripture

Discussion

Chat

Prayer Requests

Shared Notes

Shared Highlights

Ask Emmaus

Attendance

Polls

====================================================
SHARED SCRIPTURE
====================================================

Allow members of a Ministry Room to view the same Scripture.

Support shared highlighting.

Example:

Jeremy highlights Luke 24:32.

Other Room members can see the shared highlight.

Keep personal highlights separate from Room highlights.

Do not overwrite a user's personal Bible annotations.

====================================================
LEADER CONTROLS
====================================================

Authorised Room leaders should eventually be able to:

Open a Scripture for everyone

Move the group to the current discussion section

Share an Emmaus response

Start a poll

Manage participants

View attendance

Do NOT implement synchronized Bible audio.

Do NOT implement synchronized media playback.

Do NOT implement advanced whiteboards.

Do NOT implement Emmaus Voice participating in the group.

These are explicitly OUT OF SCOPE for this version.

====================================================
ASK EMMAUS IN ROOMS
====================================================

Ask Emmaus remains available.

Allow a Room member/leader to choose to share an Ask Emmaus response with the Room.

Do not automatically expose private Ask Emmaus conversations to other Room members.

Privacy must remain the default.

====================================================
ATTENDANCE
====================================================

For authorised Ministry Rooms:

Record:

• Joined
• Left
• Meeting date
• Approximate participation duration

Attendance is a ministry administration signal.

Do not use attendance to automatically make spiritual judgments about users.

====================================================
DISCOVER
====================================================

Discover may provide the appropriate entry point for finding/joining church-authorised Rooms.

Do not duplicate My Rooms.

Discover = find something new.

My Journey → My Rooms = Rooms I already belong to.

====================================================
EXISTING ROOMS
====================================================

Audit existing Room records.

Do NOT delete them.

Migrate/classify existing Rooms into the new model safely.

If the correct type cannot be determined automatically, default conservatively and report the affected records.

====================================================
BUILD ORDER
====================================================

Do NOT attempt every advanced feature simultaneously.

Implement in this order:

STEP 1

Four Room types
Roles
Permissions
My Rooms
Create Personal Room from existing content
Invitations
Existing chat/prayer/discussion

STEP 2

LiveKit integration for authorised Ministry Rooms
Camera
Microphone
Participant presence
Join/leave
Leader starts/ends meeting

STEP 3

Shared Scripture
Shared highlighting
Shared notes
Leader navigation
Shared Ask Emmaus responses

STEP 4

Polls
Attendance
Moderation
Usage controls

Church Service Rooms remain architecture-only for now.

====================================================
DO NOT BUILD
====================================================

Do NOT build:

• Our own video engine
• Synchronized Bible audio
• Emmaus Voice group participation
• Synchronized media playback
• Advanced whiteboard
• Media Studio integration
• Full online Sunday congregation
• Unrestricted member video calls

====================================================
SUCCESS CRITERIA
====================================================

An ordinary member can be halfway through a Walk or Devotional, decide:

"I want to do this with someone."

and invite them without restarting.

A Group Leader can run that same discipleship experience as an authorised live video group.

Members cannot generate uncontrolled LiveKit costs.

Church leadership controls who can host video.

The video experience remains inside Emmaus.

Rooms feel like shared discipleship, not Zoom embedded in a church app.

Preserve existing Emmaus functionality and data throughout.
