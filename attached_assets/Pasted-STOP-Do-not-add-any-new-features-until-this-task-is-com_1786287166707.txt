This task is a WORKFLOW REDESIGN of Emmaus Groups.

The functionality is largely there, but the user experience is wrong because the page is trying to be a preparation screen, a live meeting screen and a follow-up screen all at once.

I want to redesign the experience around the natural lifecycle of a discipleship group.

====================================================
THE CORE PRINCIPLE
====================================================

Emmaus Groups is NOT a video conferencing app.

Emmaus Groups is a discipleship coordination tool.

Groups may meet:

• in someone's home
• at church
• in a coffee shop
• on WhatsApp
• on Zoom
• eventually inside Emmaus Video

The app exists to prepare people, guide the meeting, and help them afterwards.

The UI must reflect this.
