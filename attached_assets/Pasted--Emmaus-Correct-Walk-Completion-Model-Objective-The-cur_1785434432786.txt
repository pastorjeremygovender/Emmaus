# Emmaus - Correct Walk Completion Model

## Objective

The current implementation still treats Walk Complete as both:

- the final numbered lesson
- and a separate completion page.

This creates duplicate content and incorrect progress calculations.

We need to correct the underlying model, not patch the UI.

---

## Current Behaviour (Incorrect)

Lesson list:

1
2
3
4
5
6 Journey Complete

Walk Contents:

Step 1

...

Step 5

Step 6 Journey Complete

Walk Complete

Progress:

Step 6 of 6

yet the Walk is still not complete.

These are all symptoms of the same modelling issue.

---

## Correct Model

A Walk contains three content types:

1. Walk Introduction

2. Numbered Steps

3. Walk Complete

Walk Complete is NOT a numbered step.

It is its own content type.

---

## Lesson List

Display only numbered teaching steps.

Example:

1 In the Beginning

2 The Master Designer

3 The Lord of Creation

4 God Knows You

5 God Wants You Know Him

Do NOT display:

6 Journey Complete

The Continue button should automatically open Walk Complete after the final numbered lesson has been completed.

## Progress

Progress should represent completion of the numbered lessons only.

For this Walk:

5 teaching steps

Progress:

Step 1 of 5

...

Step 5 of 5

After Step 5:

Continue opens Walk Complete.

When Walk Complete is finished:

Display:

Walk Completed

100%

Completed

Do not display:

Step 6 of 6

because Walk Complete is not a lesson.

---

## Root Cause

Please inspect the progress calculation and content-generation logic.

It appears the system is currently including Walk Complete in the numbered lesson collection.

Instead:

- numbered lessons should come only from Walk Steps
- Walk Introduction is separate
- Walk Complete is separate

Each should have its own content type and should not be merged into the lesson array.
