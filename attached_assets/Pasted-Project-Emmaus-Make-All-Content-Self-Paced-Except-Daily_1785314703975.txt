Project Emmaus — Make All Content Self-Paced Except Daily Rhythm

We are changing the Emmaus progression policy.

Current behaviour applies calendar-based or one-entry-per-day limits to multiple content types.

This is no longer the approved approach.

Members should be trusted to choose their own pace.

====================================================
LOCKED PROGRESSION PRINCIPLE
====================================================

Daily Rhythm:

- remains one new entry per local calendar day;
- members cannot advance early;
- replay remains available;
- the next real published day unlocks according to the established calendar rules.

All other content:

- is self-paced;
- members may complete more than one entry in a day;
- completing one entry immediately makes the next published entry available;
- no artificial waiting until tomorrow;
- no calendar-based daily limit;
- no arbitrary pacing restrictions.

Apply this to:

- Daily Devotionals;
- Sermon Companions;
- Journeys;
- Bible Studies;
- Standalone Journeys;
- Collection-based Journeys;
- future sequential content unless explicitly configured otherwise.

Do not change Daily Rhythm.

====================================================
PERMANENT RULE
====================================================

The content type determines progression:

DAILY RHYTHM
calendar-paced

DAILY DEVOTIONAL
self-paced

SERMON COMPANION
self-paced

JOURNEY
self-paced

BIBLE STUDY
self-paced

Future content defaults to self-paced unless Pastor Jeremy explicitly chooses calendar-paced delivery.

Do not infer pacing from the existence of day numbers.

“Day 1,” “Day 2” and similar labels describe sequence, not mandatory calendar dates.

====================================================
TASK 3 — DAILY DEVOTIONALS
====================================================

Remove calendar gating from Daily Devotionals.

Current or previous behaviour may include:

- one devotional entry per day;
- “Completed for today”;
- waiting until local midnight;
- start-date-based day calculation;
- next day remaining locked.

Replace it with self-paced progression.

When a member completes an entry:

- save completion;
- immediately unlock the next published entry;
- show a clear next action;
- preserve Review and View Previous Days;
- allow the member to stop and return later.

Do not automatically force-open the next entry.

Do not auto-complete it.

The member chooses whether to continue.

====================================================
TASK 4 — SERMON COMPANIONS
====================================================

Remove one-per-day gating from Sermon Companions.

A member may read all five entries in one sitting or spread them across the week.

When an entry is completed:

- save progress;
- immediately unlock the next published entry;
- offer a gentle next action;
- preserve completed-entry review;
- preserve the Sermon Companion’s ordered flow.

Do not require Monday–Friday calendar matching.

“Five weekday devotionals” may remain descriptive copy where pastor-approved, but it must not impose technical weekday locks.

Do not alter current-week visibility rules.

====================================================
TASK 5 — JOURNEYS AND BIBLE STUDIES
====================================================

Ensure Journeys and Bible Studies are fully self-paced.

When a Journey Day or Bible Study entry is completed:

- unlock the next published entry immediately;
- update Journey progress;
- allow continued reading;
- preserve the member’s place if they leave.

Do not impose:

- one lesson per day;
- a midnight reset;
- a forced waiting period;
- calendar-derived current-day numbers.

Journey prerequisites between separate Journeys may remain where explicitly configured in the future, but do not introduce them in this task.

====================================================
TASK 6 — COMPLETION SCREEN ACTIONS
====================================================

Non-Daily-Rhythm completion screens must no longer end only with:

Back to Today’s Steps

or:

Come back tomorrow.

Use a self-paced completion footer.

For a completed entry with another eligible published entry:

You’ve completed this step.

Primary action:

Continue to Next Step

Secondary actions:

Back to Next Steps

View Previous Steps →

Use content-appropriate wording where needed:

Daily Devotional:
Continue to Next Devotional

Sermon Companion:
Continue to Next Reflection

Journey:
Continue to Next Step

Bible Study:
Continue to Next Study

Keep the wording gentle and invitational.

Do not use:

- You must continue;
- Complete the next task;
- Start Day 2 now;
- Come back tomorrow.

If there is no next published entry:

You’ve reached the end of the available content.

Primary action:

Back to Next Steps

Secondary action:

Review Previous Steps →

For completed full content:

You’ve completed this devotional/companion/journey/study.

Use the approved calm, non-gamified completion style.

====================================================
TASK 7 — TODAY’S STEPS CARD BEHAVIOUR
====================================================

Daily Rhythm remains the only card with a daily completion state such as:

Today’s time with Jesus is complete.

Come back tomorrow.

Daily Devotionals and Sermon Companions must not say:

Completed for today

when more published entries remain.

Instead show their overall progress.

Examples:

Psalms Daily Devotional

Day 2 of 7

Continue

or, after completing Day 2:

2 of 7 completed

Continue

Sermon Companion:

2 of 5 completed

Continue

If the full series is complete:

Completed

Review

Do not imply a daily lock.

====================================================
TASK 9 — PREVIOUS DAYS TERMINOLOGY
====================================================

Because non-Daily-Rhythm content is self-paced, “Previous Days” may be misleading.

Preserve the established feature but use content-appropriate labels.

Daily Rhythm:

View Previous Days →

Daily Devotional:

View Previous Entries →

Sermon Companion:

View Previous Reflections →

Journey:

View Previous Steps →

Bible Study:

View Previous Studies →

If changing terminology now would cause excessive regression risk, keep the existing shared route internally but update the visible labels through content-type configuration.

Do not change Daily Rhythm’s “View Previous Days.”

====================================================
TASK 10 — PROGRESS DATA
====================================================

Preserve all existing progress.

Do not reset:

- completed entries;
- current entry;
- start dates;
- completion dates;
- active content selection.

For self-paced content, progression should derive from:

- ordered published entries;
- completed entry IDs or numbers;
- highest valid completed position;
- next eligible published entry.

Do not derive the next entry from elapsed calendar days.

Existing startDate and completionDate fields may remain for history, but they must not gate access.

====================================================
TASK 11 — PUBLISHED ENTRY VALIDATION
====================================================

Self-paced does not mean unrestricted access to nonexistent or draft content.

The next entry becomes available only when it:

- exists;
- belongs to the correct parent content;
- is Published;
- is member-visible;
- follows the current entry in display order.

Do not expose:

- Draft entries;
- Archived entries;
- nonexistent future entries;
- entries from another series.

Clamp progression to the actual published-entry list.

====================================================
TASK 12 — DIRECT ENTRY ACCESS
====================================================

Prevent members from manually changing a URL to skip to an unpublished entry.

For self-paced content, they may access:

- any completed entry;
- the next eligible published entry;
- any earlier published entry in review mode.

Choose whether members may freely jump ahead across all published entries based on the existing sequential-content rule.

Approved default for this task:

- progression remains sequential;
- completion unlocks the next entry immediately;
- members cannot skip uncompleted entries through URL manipulation.

This preserves a coherent Journey while removing calendar restrictions.

====================================================
TASK 13 — NOTIFICATIONS
====================================================

Do not introduce new notifications.

Existing reminders remain off by default.

Daily Rhythm reminder behaviour remains unchanged.

Self-paced content must not send repeated alerts encouraging members to continue.

Room notifications remain off by default.

====================================================
TASK 14 — ADMIN EXPERIENCE
====================================================

Do not require pastors to configure a release date for every Daily Devotional, Sermon Companion or Journey entry.

Publishing an entry makes it eligible in sequence.

If a publication window already exists, preserve it.

The content may be unavailable before its publication window, but once available it is self-paced.

Daily Rhythm remains calendar-paced regardless of entry publication.

====================================================
TASK 15 — LANGUAGE PRINCIPLE
====================================================

Apply the Emmaus Invitation Principle.

The app should respect member choice.

Use wording such as:

Continue when you’re ready.

The next reflection is available.

You can keep going or return later.

Avoid:

You must complete the next lesson.

Your next task is waiting.

Stay on track.

Don’t break your streak.

Complete one every day.

Do not introduce pressure, streaks, guilt or productivity language.

====================================================
TASK 16 — SHARED PROGRESSION SERVICE
====================================================

Create or update one shared progression service.

Suggested interface:

resolveSequentialProgress({
  contentType,
  entries,
  progress,
  localDate
})

Return:

{
  pacingMode,
  currentEntry,
  nextEntry,
  completedEntries,
  isCurrentComplete,
  isParentComplete,
  canContinue,
  canReview,
  isWaitingForCalendar
}

Expected:

Daily Rhythm:
isWaitingForCalendar may be true.

All self-paced content:
isWaitingForCalendar must always be false.

Use this shared service on:

- Today’s Steps;
- Next Steps;
- content detail screens;
- completion footers;
- direct-route guards;
- progress APIs.

Do not keep competing progression calculations.

====================================================
TASK 17 — BACKWARD COMPATIBILITY
====================================================

Existing members who were previously waiting for tomorrow must immediately be able to continue to the next published entry after deployment.

Do not require them to restart the content.

Do not mark waiting entries complete.

Do not skip an entry.

Use their existing completed progress to resolve the next available entry.
