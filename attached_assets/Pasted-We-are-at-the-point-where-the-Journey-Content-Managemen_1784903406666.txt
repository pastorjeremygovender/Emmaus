==================================================
1. CHANGE "DAY" TO "STEP"
==================================================

Internally replace every "Day" with "Step".

This allows Emmaus to support:

• Daily devotionals
• Bible studies
• Courses
• Reading plans
• Marriage journeys
• Volunteer training
• Leadership training
• Children's journeys

The user interface may still display "Day 1" where appropriate, but the database entity should always be Step.
