DARK MODE

The dark page background is good, but the cards do not feel like a cohesive dark theme.

Design intention:
- Calm
- Warm
- Gentle
- Premium
- Highly readable
- Clearly recognisable as Emmaus

Requirements:

- Keep a deep charcoal/navy background rather than pure black.
- Do not use nearly black inner cards against pale daytime outer cards.
- Create dark-mode-specific versions of all section surfaces.
- Use softer elevated charcoal inner cards.
- Give each outer section card a muted dark tint that preserves its identity:
  - Start Here: warm gold
  - Sermon Companions: blue
  - Daily Devotionals: violet
  - Walks: green
  - Journeys: its existing colour identity
- Maintain clear visual separation between the page background, outer section card and inner content card.
- Keep section headings and coloured indicators recognisable.
- Use warm off-white for primary text rather than stark white.
- Ensure secondary text remains clearly readable.
- Improve the contrast of search fields, borders, disabled buttons, remove icons, completion indicators and empty-state controls.
- Disabled controls must look disabled but must not become illegible.
- Give the bottom navigation a stable dark surface. It must not look transparent, blurred or mismatched.
- Apply the theme consistently across:
  - Today’s Steps
  - Daily Rhythm
  - My Bible
  - Bible reading
  - Discover
  - Walks
  - Journeys
  - Devotionals
  - Sermon Companions
  - Ask Emmaus
  - Groups
  - Settings and other modals
  - Admin screens only where they already support the user theme
- Do not alter layouts, content, navigation or functionality.
- Do not remove the existing light theme.
- Build shared semantic colour tokens rather than styling individual screens independently.
- Check every component for hard-coded white, black and light-mode background values.

TEXT SIZE

The Standard, Large and Extra Large controls currently have no visible effect. Repair the feature completely.

Requirements:

- Apply the selected size immediately, without a refresh.
- Persist the preference for the signed-in user.
- Restore it when Emmaus is reopened or the user signs in again.
- If account persistence temporarily fails, retain the setting locally and synchronise safely later.
- Use a root attribute, CSS custom properties or shared typography tokens consumed by the full user-facing app.
- Do not use browser zoom or transform: scale.
- Do not enlarge every element indiscriminately.
- Preserve the hierarchy between body text, captions, buttons, headings and display headings.

Use these approximate scales:

- Standard: 100%
- Large: 112.5%
- Extra Large: 125%

Apply the preference throughout:

- Daily Rhythm content
- Bible text
- Walk and Journey content
- Devotionals
- Sermon Companions
- Ask Emmaus answers
- Groups
- Settings
- Buttons and labels where appropriate
- Navigation where larger text will not damage usability

Mobile layout requirements:

- Cards and buttons must grow vertically with their content.
- Never clip or overlap text.
- Avoid unnecessary title truncation.
- Long titles must wrap where practical.
- Bottom navigation must remain usable.
- Modals must remain within the viewport and scroll correctly.
- Controls must not disappear offscreen.
- Bible reading must remain comfortable at Extra Large.
- Minimum touch-target sizes must be preserved.
