TASK 2 — CANONICAL ELIGIBILITY RULE
====================================================

A Sermon Companion belongs in the member Sermon Companions tab when:

- companion status is Published;
- companion is not Archived;
- member visibility is enabled;
- at least one companion entry is Published;
- publication window is valid, where used.

It does not need to be marked This Week’s Sermon to appear.

The current-week flag only determines whether it appears in the featured top section.

It must not determine whether it appears at all.
