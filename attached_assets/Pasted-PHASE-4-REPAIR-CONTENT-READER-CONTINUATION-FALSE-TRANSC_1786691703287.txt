==================================================
POST-TTS MIC GUARD
==================================================

Introduce a safe post-TTS transition.

After TTS finishes:

1. fully stop/release playback
2. allow a short configurable settling period
3. restart microphone
4. ignore audio until genuine speech onset is detected

Do NOT blindly record/transcribe immediately after TTS ends.

The settling period should be just long enough to avoid echo/residual audio, not so long that conversation feels sluggish.
