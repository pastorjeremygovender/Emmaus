=====================================================
CORRECTION GOAL

Correct the hierarchy and opening flow of the Walk with Jesus member experience.

The current implementation incorrectly allows another recommendation, such as this week’s sermon, to appear above 15 Minutes with Jesus.

This must change.

15 Minutes with Jesus is the heartbeat of Emmaus.

It must always appear first on the Walk with Jesus screen.

The member home screen must follow a fixed, trustworthy discipleship rhythm rather than allowing a recommendation engine to rearrange the primary content hierarchy.

=====================================================

The Walk with Jesus home screen must always use this order:

1. 15 Minutes with Jesus
2. This Week’s Sermon Devotional
3. Daily Devotional
4. Continue Your Journeys
5. This Week progress

This order is fixed.

Do not place any other content above 15 Minutes with Jesus.

Do not add a Browse Journeys button or Browse Journeys section to the Walk screen.

Users can browse and manage Journeys through the existing Journeys tab in the bottom navigation.

Do not duplicate Journey discovery on the Walk screen.

Display only:

Walk with Jesus

1. Application launches or receives a full page load.

- After a fresh app launch
- After opening the installed app
- After beginning a new browser session
- After a full page reload of the member application
- Before the member home screen following login, when it has not already appeared for that launch

Do not show a blank white screen before or after it.

=====================================================
15 MINUTES WITH JESUS

15 Minutes with Jesus must always be the first and most prominent card.

It must appear directly beneath the greeting.

It is not one optional recommendation among many.

It is the central daily rhythm of Emmaus.

Possible states:

- Begin Day 1
- Ready for today
- Continue today
- Completed today
- Available tomorrow

If the current day is unfinished, the main action should be:

Begin

or

Continue

If today’s session has been completed, the card must remain first and display:

Today’s time with Jesus is complete.

Come back tomorrow for the next step.

Allow the user to review today’s session again.

Do not hide the card after completion.

Do not replace it with a sermon, devotional or ordinary Journey.

A user may complete only one new Day of 15 Minutes with Jesus per local calendar day.

The user may:

- Reopen today’s completed Day
- Review any previous Day
- Reread Scripture
- Repeat the reflection
- Repeat the prayer
- Review their own responses
- Listen again where audio exists

The user may not:

- Unlock the next new Day on the same local date
- Bypass the limit by refreshing
- Bypass the limit from another device
- Bypass the limit through client-side clock manipulation where server validation is available

The next Day becomes available on the next local calendar date, not after a rolling 24-hour timer.

Do not show a countdown clock.
