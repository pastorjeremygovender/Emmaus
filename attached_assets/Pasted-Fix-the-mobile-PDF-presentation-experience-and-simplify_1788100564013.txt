Fix the mobile PDF presentation experience and simplify joining an active meeting to one action.

A. FIX PRESENTED PDFs ON MOBILE

Current confirmed behaviour:
1. The leader presents a PDF.
2. The participant sees a presentation card.
3. The participant taps Open PDF.
4. Emmaus launches another browser/PWA context.
5. A splash/loading screen appears.
6. The PDF eventually opens in an external browser-style viewer.
7. The participant is taken away from the Emmaus meeting and has difficulty returning.

This is not acceptable meeting behaviour.

Required behaviour:
- Display presented PDFs inside Emmaus.
- Do not open a new browser tab.
- Do not launch a second PWA instance.
- Do not navigate to a raw storage URL.
- Do not replace the current meeting route.
- Do not disconnect or recreate the LiveKit meeting connection.
- Do not show the Emmaus splash screen when opening a presented PDF.

Use an in-app PDF presentation viewer.

For mobile, do not depend on a raw PDF inside an iframe because Android browsers and PWAs handle this inconsistently.

Use PDF.js or an equivalent reliable in-app renderer to:
- load the PDF from its authenticated URL
- render pages within the Emmaus presentation surface
- fit each page to the phone width
- preserve the page aspect ratio
- allow vertical page scrolling
- allow zoom where practical
- show the current page and total page count
- provide Previous and Next page controls
- provide a clear Close or Back to group control

While viewing the PDF:
- Keep Mute/Unmute visible.
- Keep Raise/Lower hand visible.
- Keep Camera on/off visible for video meetings.
- Keep Leave visible.
- Do not cover the PDF with the controls.
- Keep the participant connected to the live meeting.
- Continue receiving audio.
- Continue receiving presentation and meeting-state updates.

The participant should be able to close the PDF viewer and return to the exact Group screen without reconnecting.

If the PDF cannot be rendered:
- Show a helpful error.
- Offer Download PDF or Open externally as a secondary fallback only.
- Clearly warn that Open externally will leave Emmaus.
- Never make external opening the primary action.

Use a signed or authenticated file URL that PDF.js can fetch. Fix storage CORS or authorization correctly if necessary. Do not expose a private permanent URL.

B. OPTIONAL SYNCHRONIZED PDF PAGES

If the leader changes the presented PDF page:
- Broadcast the current page number in real time.
- Participants following the presentation move to that page immediately.
- A participant may turn off “Follow presenter” and browse independently.
- They can resume following without refreshing.

This must use the existing authoritative presentation state and real-time meeting channel.

C. REMOVE THE THREE-STAGE JOIN PROCESS

Current process:
1. Tap Join Meeting.
2. Tap Join Live Audio.
3. Choose an option and tap Join gathering.

This is too much and contains duplicate join actions.

For an active audio meeting, show one primary button:

“Join Meeting”

When tapped:
1. Request microphone permission if it has not already been decided.
2. Connect to the active LiveKit audio meeting.
3. Publish the microphone using the user’s last known choice or the default state.
4. Update the participant as joined.
5. Display the live meeting controls.

Do not show a second “Join Live Audio” button.
Do not require a third “Join gathering” tap.
Do not render duplicate meeting-entry cards.

The “Meeting in Progress” card must be the single source of entry.

D. DEFAULT AUDIO JOIN BEHAVIOUR

For an audio meeting:
- Join with the microphone on by default unless the user previously chose muted or listen-only.
- The browser’s own first-time microphone permission prompt is unavoidable and may appear after tapping Join Meeting.
- Once permission is granted, complete the connection automatically.
- Do not make the user tap Join again after granting permission.

If microphone permission is denied:
- Do not leave the user stuck.
- Automatically offer:
  - Join listen-only
  - Instructions to restore microphone permission
- Joining listen-only must not require camera or microphone permission.

E. VIDEO MEETING JOIN

For a video meeting:
- Use the same single Join Meeting action.
- Default to microphone on and camera off unless the user’s saved preference says otherwise.
- Request only the permissions needed for the chosen state.
- Allow camera activation after joining.
- Do not require a separate Join Live Video button followed by Join gathering.

F. DEVICE CHECK IS OPTIONAL

Remove “Check your devices” as a compulsory stage.

Device check should be available only as a secondary option such as:
“Check audio before joining”

It must not sit between the user and the normal Join Meeting action.

For audio meetings, it checks audio only.
For video meetings, it may check microphone and camera separately.

After an optional check:
- provide one Join Meeting button
- do not return the user to another join stage

G. FIX CONTRADICTORY MEETING STATE

The screenshot shows “You are in this meeting” while the device-check/join panel is still visible.

This must never happen.

Use one explicit connection state:
- not_joined
- requesting_permission
- connecting
- joined
- reconnecting
- failed
- left

UI rules:
- `not_joined`: show Join Meeting
- `requesting_permission`: show Allow microphone
- `connecting`: show Joining…
- `joined`: show live controls and Leave
- `reconnecting`: show Reconnecting…
- `failed`: show a clear Retry or Listen only option
- `left`: return to Join Meeting state

Never show the pre-join device panel after the participant is joined.
Never mark the participant as joined before the LiveKit connection succeeds.

H. PREVENT DUPLICATE CONNECTIONS

The one-tap join action must be idempotent:
- Disable it while connecting.
- Ignore rapid duplicate taps.
- Do not create multiple tokens.
- Do not create duplicate LiveKit participants.
- Do not publish duplicate microphone tracks.
- Reuse the shared room instance across Group, Discussion, Bible and PDF screens.

I. MOBILE LAYOUT

On mobile:
- Show one clear Join Meeting button in the Meeting in Progress card.
- Remove the separate “Live Audio is active / Join Live Audio” card.
- Avoid another large device-check card in the main Group page.
- Keep the meeting page calm and compact.
- Ask Emmaus must not overlap meeting actions.
- All actions must remain inside the viewport.

ACCEPTANCE TESTS

One-action audio join:
- Open a Group with an active audio meeting.
- Tap Join Meeting once.
- Grant microphone permission.
- Participant connects automatically without another Emmaus button tap.
- No Join Live Audio or Join gathering step appears.
- Mute, hand raise and Leave controls appear.

Returning participant:
- Open an audio meeting with microphone permission already granted.
- Tap Join Meeting once.
- Connect directly.

Permission denied:
- Tap Join Meeting and deny microphone permission.
- Emmaus clearly offers Join listen-only.
- Joining listen-only requires only one additional deliberate choice.
- Camera permission is never requested.

Presented PDF on mobile:
- Leader presents a PDF.
- Participant sees it immediately without refreshing.
- Participant opens it inside Emmaus.
- No new browser tab, second PWA, splash screen or raw PDF URL appears.
- Participant continues hearing live audio.
- Meeting controls remain visible.
- Participant closes the viewer and returns to the Group without reconnecting.

PDF failure:
- Simulate an unavailable PDF.
- Show a useful error and secondary external/download fallback.
- Do not silently open another app.

Meeting state:
- At no point show “You are in this meeting” together with pre-join controls.
- Rapidly tapping Join does not create duplicate connections.

Before finishing:
- Identify why PDFs were routed to an external browser context.
- Identify why three separate join actions existed.
- Consolidate all joining into one shared `joinMeeting()` action.
- Report the files changed and the result of every acceptance test.
